# fields: expr
Split this referring expression into its head noun and its atomic distinguishing details:
{expr}

Rules:
- The head noun goes on the first line, prefixed "HEAD: ".
- Then one detail per line, prefixed "CLAUSE: ". Each must be a single checkable predicate about the object, written so that it can follow the words "the object ..." (for example "CLAUSE: has a blue stripe", "CLAUSE: is lying on its side", "CLAUSE: is to the left of the laptop").
- Keep the wording of each detail verbatim from the expression; do not paraphrase or add information.
- A conjunction of two independent properties is two details.
- Output nothing else.
