# Survey B — abstention, unanswerable-question detection, calibration and abstention-aware training outside grounding

Author: `SurveyB`. Date: 2026-09-16. Scope: the general VLM/LLM literature a grounding-rejection method should borrow from or be measured against. Grounding-native rejection (gRefCOCO, GroundingME, RC-GRPO, OpenRef) is SurveyA's; presence heads / DETR no-object / mechanistic refusal directions are SurveyC's. Where those touch this slice they are cited, not re-derived.

Sources: arXiv HTML or abs pages, or the PDF pulled and read with pypdf when the HTML fetch garbled a table (RefBench-PRO, POPE, TDIUC, Hallucination Tax, MM-UPD, TIAR, KARL, MM-AQA, HARMONY, CAP). About 85 web calls used. Labels: `[VERIFIED: id]` = read the full text or the page carrying the number; `[LIKELY: abs]` = abstract only; `[P: file]` = this project's measurement; `[DERIVED]` = arithmetic on verified numbers.

Negative-type vocabulary used throughout (charter): **easy** = category absent from the image / question about nothing in the image / options shuffled; **hard** = the object or a near-match is present and one clause of the expression is false.

## 0. The numbers that change decisions (details in the tables below)

1. **Yes/no presence and grounding rejection are already measured on the same items, once.** RefBench-PRO Table 5 (`[VERIFIED: 2512.06276 PDF]`): Qwen3-VL-8B reject-as-grounding **15.8**, reject-as-yes/no **64.2**; Qwen2.5-VL-7B **3.1 / 55.1**; InternVL3-8B **46.8 / 17.4** (the only model where the yes/no form is *worse*); Qwen2-VL-7B 28.5 / 51.7; InternVL3.5-8B — / 49.2. Paper's own reading: "performance remains close to random chance (approximately 50%) under the binary classification setting." So on hard negatives the yes/no form is not solved either; what is solved (AMBER existence F1 94.5 for GPT-4V, POPE) is the *category-absent* form. The "presence is solved, rejection is zero" contrast holds only across negative types, not across output formats.
2. **The information is usually there; the output policy does not read it.** TRAPSBench (`[VERIFIED: 2608.13167]`): linear probes decode answerability at **0.91 AUROC** while the best spontaneous restraint score is **0.292**, and a single-layer steering direction "causally induces or suppresses abstention". HALP (`[VERIFIED: 2603.05465]`): a probe on the query-token state *before any token is generated* reaches **0.89 mean AUROC** across 8 VLMs (0.935 Gemma3-12B, 0.915 Qwen2.5-VL-7B) on a 10k mix that includes POPE and AMBER existence items. Same structure as our p(null) result (`[P: notes/ABSTAIN-SIGNAL.md]`).
3. **But the visual side of that information is weaker than the textual side.** TRAPSBench: "models detect textual impossibility about 4× more readily than missing visual evidence". MM-AQA (`[VERIFIED: 2604.14799 PDF]`): frontier VLMs "abstain when image or text evidence is absent, but attempt reconciliation with degraded or contradictory evidence" — base unanswerable-abstention 1.2 % (Claude Sonnet 4.5), 4.9 % (GPT-5), 4.0 % (Qwen2.5-VL-32B) on Abstain-MMMU. FPCO-Dialog (`[VERIFIED: 2609.03331]`): false-premise correction 0.56 for identity errors, 0.41 attribute, **0.15 location**. The hard-negative regime is the one the literature also fails on.
4. **RL with a binary correctness reward removes whatever abstention existed.** Hallucination Tax (`[VERIFIED: 2505.13988 PDF, Fig. 2]`): Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct refusal on UMWP **0.30 → 0.08**, SelfAware 0.27 → 0.09, SUM 0.11 → 0.02 after RFT on solvable math. AbstentionBench (`[VERIFIED: 2506.09038]`): reasoning fine-tuning degrades abstention **24 % on average**; "model scale has almost no effect". DCPO (`[VERIFIED: 2603.09117]`): GRPO on Qwen3-8B raises AIME24 ECE 0.461 → 0.515. Mixing **10 %** unanswerables back restores refusal (0.08 → 0.85) at GSM8K −0.05.
5. **A fixed positive abstention reward collapses to always-refuse; a neutral one with shaped advantage does not.** AWA-RL (`[VERIFIED: alphaxiv 2607.10738]`): "even a tiny static refusal reward (like 0.05) caused the model to collapse and refuse nearly 100% of questions". TruthRL ternary (+1/0/−1) 37.2 truthfulness vs binary 20.8 (`[VERIFIED: 2509.25760]`). TIAR: inversion weight λ = 0.3 "causes a collapse in both metrics"; λ = 1.0 keeps accuracy 72.6 vs 72.7 (`[VERIFIED: 2605.25850 PDF]`). Rewarding Intellectual Humility: r_abs ∈ [−0.25, 0.3] is the working band (`[LIKELY: abs 2601.20126]`). This is the literature behind RC-GRPO's over-refusal penalty.
6. **Abstention at ≤5-point cost on answerables is routine when unknowns are labelled by the model's own sampling; ≥6–21 points when negatives are imposed at high ratio.** Alignment-for-Honesty Confidence-Verb: prudence 0 → 58.9 at TriviaQA accuracy 73.71 → 73.34 (`[VERIFIED: 2312.07000]`). IDK-token: TriviaQA precision 52.0 → 72.5 at recall 52.0 → 52.0 (`[VERIFIED: 2412.06676]`). Abstain-R1: U-Ref 9.4 → 68.1 with answerable accuracy 48.8 → **57.2** (`[VERIFIED: 2604.17073]`). Against: LLaVA-1.5-7B SFT on Visual-Idk −17.9 IK-IK (ORPO −5.5) (`[VERIFIED: 2604.26419]`); GroundingME 2:1 negative SFT −21 Discriminative (`[P: charter]`).
7. **No verbalized or self-verified signal reaches AUROC 0.85 in any VLM setting; trained probes, our decision-token logit in narrow regimes, image-ablation contrasts and localized attention do.** Table in §3.
8. **The detection analogue of our removal pipeline already works as a reward.** CFCamo (`[VERIFIED: abs 2606.11231]`), Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct: counterfactual paired reward (detect on the original, abstain on the inpainted target-absent copy) gives pair accuracy **80.0–90.8 %** vs **1.4–5.2 %** without the coupling, with S_α **+3.7 pp** on target-present images.

Conclusions. (a) Above 0.85 is reached only by (1) supervised probes/scorers on hidden states or attention, (2) our own decision-token logit when the expression is a single checkable clause, (3) localized attention for box *correctness*; the image-ablation contrast is at 0.86–0.88 for existence claims and 0.77 for relations. (b) Every verbalized, self-verified or sampling-consistency signal in a VLM sits at 0.55–0.82, and the two papers that tested evidence *removal* (Explicit knobs; TRAPSBench) show verbalized confidence does not move when the evidence is taken away. (c) No signal has been measured on hard grounding negatives (present near-match, one clause false) except ours (0.298 / 0.685). (d) The probe results (HALP, TRAPSBench) are the direct test of whether our 0.298 is a representation failure or a read-out failure: a probe on the query-token state at the decision position on GME Rejection vs positives, trained on removals, costs one forward pass per item and no GPU training beyond a logistic regression.

## 5. Analysis (4): what transfers to grounding and what does not

Grounding differs in three ways the literature mostly does not face: the output is a coordinate string with no confidence token; the negative is a partially matching object, not a missing fact; the benchmark scoring already credits the null (and parse failures), so the eval-incentive lever is inert.

**Transfers (with the number that says so)**

| From | To grounding | Why it should carry |
|---|---|---|
| Ternary / neutral abstention reward with the abstention advantage scaled by the group's pass rate (TruthRL, TIAR λ(1 − 2p̂), KARL, AWA-RL R_base(q)) | RC-GRPO already has forced-None rollouts + over-refusal penalty; add per-item p̂ from the verifier (IoU pass rate across G rollouts) to scale the null advantage; zero fixed bonus for null | AWA-RL 0.05 → 100 % refusal; TIAR coupled −0.76; Abstain-R1 false-abstain −1.0 gives +8.4 acc |
| Counterfactual paired reward (CFCamo) | our removal pipeline is exactly the pair: reward = box on original ∧ null on the removal; PA 80–91 % on Qwen3-VL-4B with S_α +3.7 | same model family, same edit type; the pair holds the expression fixed, which is what separated 0.964 from 0.298 in our data |
| 10 % unanswerable mix restores refusal at ≤0.05 (Hallucination Tax); 0.2 + 0.2 in MM-UPD; 67 % cost −21 in GroundingME | keep the negative fraction at 10–30 % in any SFT/RL mix | monotone cost with fraction across three papers |
| `<REJ>` token replacing `<LOC>` (PostAlign) and [IDK] with an FP regulariser | a dedicated null token with an explicit false-null penalty term is the cheapest SFT form; PostAlign never tested hard negatives, so this is the experiment that is missing | IDK: recall cost 0 on TriviaQA, −15 on PopQA — expect the cost on Limited (tiny objects), where we already false-abstain 13 % |
| Pre-generation hidden-state probe (HALP 0.89, TRAPSBench 0.91) and steering direction | probe the query-token / decision-position state on GME Rejection vs positives; if ≥ 0.85 the failure is read-out and steering or a logit bias is the fix; if ≈ 0.3 like p(null), it is perception and only data helps | TRAPSBench got a causal steering direction from one layer |
| Image-ablation likelihood contrast (BCEA ℓ(x) − ℓ(∅); VL-Calibration KL under image perturbation) | score a box by log p(box | image) − log p(box | region masked / blank); training-free; separates "box from the image" from "box from the prior" — the prior-driven "box on the hole" cases are what a p(null) threshold cannot clear | BCEA existence 0.86–0.88; SIEVES localisation score transfers across models |
| Alignment-for-Honesty / Visual-Idk self-labelling | do not label positives as abstain-eligible by hand: sample k boxes, treat items the model cannot ground at k as unknowns; mixing those with true negatives reduced the tax from −17.9 (SFT) to −5.5 (ORPO) | the two best-cost entries in §3 are self-labelled |
| Triple scoring + abstention rate by stratum (2609.17686), Kalai's confidence-target instruction | report Rejection with false-null rate on Limited/Spatial/Discriminative, not the single 201-item number; the −0.304 correlation between GroundingME Rejection and OpenRef N3R `[P: charter]` is what stratified reporting exposes | — |

**Does not transfer, or transfers with a caveat**

| Item | Why not |
|---|---|
| Verbalized confidence and drawn-box self-verification | r = 0.22 (IVT), MCC 0.11 (MM-AQA), >85 % on wrong answers (2506.18183); HyperClick needed a Gaussian target to get ECE 0.25 and still has no absent-target test |
| "Knowledge boundary" framing (unknown = fact not in weights) | the grounding negative is present-but-wrong-clause; MM-AQA shows models *reconcile* contradictory evidence rather than abstain, FPCO-Dialog location 0.15; every self-labelling recipe assumes unanswerables are items the model gets wrong when sampled, but a hard negative is an item the model gets *consistently* wrong with high confidence — sampling will not find it |
| Yes/no presence as a proxy or as a training signal | RefBench-PRO: 46.8–64.2 on the same items ≈ chance; AMBER/POPE saturation is category-level; training on POPE-style negatives teaches the easy regime that Qwen3-VL-4B already has (gRefCOCO N-acc 77.2 `[P: charter]`) |
| "None of the above" option engineering (MM-UPD option / instruction settings move UPD by tens of points) | `{"bbox_2d": null}` *is* that option and is in the prompt; 0/201 `[P]`. The prompt lever is already pulled |

| Scale and thinking as levers | AbstentionBench, AbstainEQA, GroundingME (2B–235B all 0.0) agree scale does nothing; 2506.18183 says thinking raises overconfidence |
| Hidden-state probes as the *deployed* mechanism | HALP/HaloProbe are detectors on easy negatives; nobody has shown a probe-gated null on hard grounding negatives; it is a diagnostic here, not yet a method |
