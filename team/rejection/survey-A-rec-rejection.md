## 1. Master table (one row per paper, chronological)

Negative types: **CA** = category absent / expression from another image; **ATTR-LLM** = LLM-perturbed attribute or relation of a *present* object; **ATTR-H** = human-written or human-edited false clause about a present object; **EDIT** = edited image (object removed or replaced); **NEG** = linguistic negation ("not wearing"). Easy = CA; hard = ATTR-* / EDIT.

## 5. Metric incompatibilities and known scoring artifacts

| metric | where | definition | what it silently rewards / hides |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-acc | gRefCOCO, GREC, Ground-V, UniRef-UAV, RC-GRPO | share of no-target samples with empty output (0 boxes / <50 px) | an always-refuse model scores 100; must be read with T-acc / P-acc, which most MLLM papers omit (RC-GRPO is the exception: SFT arm N-acc 95.4 at P-acc 30.1) |
| T-acc / P-acc | gRefCOCO (ReLA 96-98), RC-GRPO | share of target samples not refused | the only honest cost column; absent from GSVA, HumanRef, Ref-R1, Ground-V tables |
| Pr@(F1=1, IoU≥0.5) | GREC, RC-GRPO, UniRef-UAV | per-sample F1 must be exactly 1; no-target → F1=1 iff zero boxes | folds rejection into the headline (no-target ≈ 12% of gRefCOCO val); set-level strictness makes it incomparable with Acc@0.5 |
| GroundingME Rejection | 2512.17495 | Acc on 201 items; correct = literal `{"bbox_2d": null}` | **unparseable output counted correct** `[P]`; 201 items → ±4-7 CI; 50 per sub-axis; weak models' non-zero scores are suspect (§2) |
| Rejection Score | HumanRef, Rex-Thinker | share of expressions with zero boxes | parse failure = rejection `[LIKELY]`; Rex-Thinker without hints reached 71.7 by over-rejecting, and the authors say so |
| RejAcc | RefBench-PRO | no box **or** an explicit absence statement | judge unspecified; the yes/no probe (55-64%) shows the model is near chance on the underlying question, so a lenient judge can inflate this freely |
| **N3R** | OpenRef | mean over negatives of ∏(1 − c_k); c_k = mean coordinate-token probability for MLLMs | a *soft* score: one low-confidence box still earns most of the credit; no parse rule; category-noun negatives (easy) are pooled with colour/orientation negatives (hard) with no per-type breakdown. This is why Qwen3-VL-8B is 84.6 here and 0.0 on GroundingME (corr −0.304 across models `[P: CHARTER.md]`) |
| pairwise Recall@k / AUROC | FineCops-Ref | rank positive above its paired negative by confidence | no abstention is ever emitted; a model that always boxes can score well; RC-GRPO had to define a string-based N-acc on the same data |
| DF1 | HumanRef | F1 with a density penalty for multi-instance | rejection is reported separately; DF1 alone can rise while rejection falls |
| AP (FULL/PRES/ABS), AP-descr vs AP-descr-pos | D3, OmniLabel | detection AP; absence handled via FPs and score thresholds | ABS in D3 is *negation*, not no-target; NMS-AP (OVDEval) shows plain AP over-credits multi-label hedging by up to 20 points |
| Empty Accuracy / error rate / instr. acc | AgroVG / KnowDR-REC | zero boxes; share with a box; yes/no | KnowDR-REC's two settings disagree by 20-30 points on the same model, same direction as RefBench-PRO's yes/no probe |
| rIoU / mRR, CMS, Ref-ZOM Acc | R-RefCOCO, HalluSegBench, Ref-ZOM | mask-based | not comparable to box metrics; CMS is an error score (lower better) |

Cross-cutting artifacts. (1) Format: a base model that never emits the refusal string scores ~0 on N-acc regardless of what it knows (Qwen2.5-VL-7B 6.4 vs Qwen3-VL-4B 77.2 on identical data), so pre/post deltas on such models (RC-GRPO's +65) overstate what the method does on a model that already refuses (+8.1). (2) Rephrasing rejection as yes/no gives 46-64% — "close to random" `[V: 2512.06276]` — so the deficit is verification, not format; but the same probe shows format alone accounts for 40-50 points of the gap between 3.1 and 55.1. (3) Unparseable-as-rejection is present in at least three scorers (GroundingME `[P]`, HumanRef `[LIKELY]`, any zero-box N-acc harness). (4) The same model gets different numbers from the same authors (RefBench-PRO 71.4 vs PR-Bench 59.4 `[P]`). (5) No rejection benchmark except GroundingME (100 items, 91%) has a human baseline.

## 6. The five most influential papers in this line (my judgement)

1. **gRefCOCO / GRES (2306.00968)** — invented the no-target sample and the N-acc / T-acc pair; every MLLM rejection number since is either on it or in its vocabulary, and its unpublished rule-1 / rule-2 ratio is why "easy vs hard" is still confounded there.
2. **FP-RefCOCO / SESAME (2312.08366)** — first LLM-perturbed false-premise set and the "catastrophic forgetting of *seeing*" diagnosis: the base LLaVA could already say no, grounding fine-tuning removed it; still the best prior model on 2026's edited-image test.
3. **HumanRef / RexSeek → Rex-Thinker (2503.08507, 2506.04034)** — the first hard-negative rejection subset with a clean before/after (0 → 54 → 68) and the first evidence that CoT buys rejection at zero positive cost; also the clearest demonstration of over-rejection gaming a zero-box metric.
4. **GroundingME (2512.17495)** — the cliff: the same models at 77-85 on gRefCOCO no-target score 0.0 on human-edited long descriptions, and in-domain 97 becomes OOD 28 at a 20-point positive cost. It defined the target this project is aiming at.
5. **RC-GRPO (2608.04698)** — the first rejection recipe with an explicit over-refusal penalty, a refusal-forcing rollout, a general-capability regression table, and an honest one-knob trade-off curve (α); the natural next experiment (run it on L4) has not been run.

GSVA (`[REJ]` token) and OpenRef/MCC (training-free count check) are the runners-up.

## 7. What I could not verify, and open disputes

- PR-Bench Reject construction and scoring, and its full leaderboard: HTML truncated, PDF >10 MB. "No model above 47.5" and "Qwen3-VL-8B 59.4" are the project's own PDF reading `[P]`, not mine.
- GroundingME's synthetic negatives for the data-mixture experiment: the paper says only "modifying the description"; whether they are L1, L2 or L4-style is unknown, which matters for interpreting 0 → 27.9.
- gRefCOCO rule-1 vs rule-2 share of no-target samples: not in 2306.00968, 2308.16182 or 2601.05244.
- OpenRef per-type negative counts and whether perturbed objects remain visible: not in v3.
- KnowDR-REC per-model negative columns: read from a truncated HTML table, mapping marked `[LIKELY]`.
- RA-RFT (2511.23151) numbers: abstract only.
- Whether GroundingME thinking-mode outputs are parsed with the same scorer as non-thinking ones (would matter for the 4.5-9.5 figures): not stated.
- Dispute with the charter's framing: the charter calls gRefCOCO negatives "category absent". Rule 1 of gRefCOCO is an attribute mismatch on a present category, so gRefCOCO is a *mixture* of L1 and L1.5 and the 77-85 N-acc there is an upper bound on easy-negative skill, not a clean easy-only number.
