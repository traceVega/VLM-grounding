## 0. Short version for the researchers

**Sub-areas I consider hopeless for a 2026-2027 top-venue paper unless you have something structurally new.**
- GRPO plus IoU reward for referring expression comprehension (REC) on a Qwen2.5-VL base, evaluated on RefCOCO. Roughly ten near-identical papers since March 2025 (Section 1A). The base models were trained on RefCOCO train (Section 3, item 2), so the benchmark measures nothing.
- Crop-and-zoom "thinking with images" trained with GRPO and evaluated on V* Bench and HR-Bench. Dozen-plus papers (Section 1B), and two 2026 audits show the tool calls often do not causally matter and the gains can be distilled into a single pass (Section 5).
- Bolting a SAM/SAM2 decoder to an MLLM for referring segmentation and reporting RefCOCO cIoU and ReasonSeg (Section 1D). SAM 3 with a text prompt is now the baseline to beat, and Qwen3-VL-Seg already exists.
- Another GUI-grounding data-scaling report on ScreenSpot-Pro (Section 1C). ScreenSpot-Pro went from 18.9 (Jan 2025) to about 62 single-shot (Qwen3-VL flagship, Nov 2025), about 73 with zoom-in in tech reports (MAI-UI-32B, Seed1.8, Gemini-3-Pro, Dec 2025 [VERIFIED by Verifier V-099: 2512.22047]) and the low 80s on the community leaderboard with multi-step zoom agents (self-submitted, undated [Verifier V-026/V-027]); UI-Venus-2-27B single model 74.1 [VERIFIED V-085]. Industrial data engines and zoom-in agents drive this; an academic team cannot match either, and single-pass and agentic numbers are no longer comparable.
- DPO variants for object hallucination on LLaVA-1.5 evaluated on POPE/CHAIR (Section 1F).
- tIoU-reward GRPO for temporal video grounding on Charades-STA (Section 1G).

**Crowded, but with room if you bring a specific thing.**
- Rejection / ungroundable queries: GroundingME reports that nearly all of 25 models score about 0% on rejection [VERIFIED: 2512.17495]; RC-GRPO and GREx exist but the problem is far from solved and the trade-off with positive accuracy is real.
- Faithfulness of grounded reasoning: methods exist (Faithful-MR1, attention-guided fine-tuning, iVGR) but nobody has a causal evaluation that the community trusts; the "illusion of visual tool-use" audit [VERIFIED: 2608.06270] gives you the tool to build one.
- Evaluation: label errors, contamination and saturation are documented (Section 5) but no widely adopted clean protocol exists; a benchmark paper here needs a new *measurement*, not another 1,000 hard examples.
- Spatial reasoning: data synthesis is crowded, but transfer from templated indoor data to open-world scenes is documented as poor [VERIFIED: 2512.19683] and nobody has an accepted explanation.

**Under-explored, in my judgement [SPECULATION].**
- Coordinate / output-format representation as a controlled variable (box vs point vs mask-as-text vs quantized tokens) across the same base model and data. Rex-Omni touched it, no controlled study exists that I could find.
- Grounding metrics vs downstream utility: whether IoU improvements translate into agent or robot task success. Every RL paper assumes it.
- Mechanistic accounts of where localization lives in the network and how cheaply it can be surfaced, extending the Frankenstein analysis [VERIFIED: 2602.12395].
- Calibrated abstention on grounding with a confidence that actually correlates with correctness (current correlation reported at about 0.22 [VERIFIED: 2606.13156]).

## 1. Crowded-area map

### 1A. GRPO / RL with IoU rewards for referring expression comprehension and detection

Representative papers.
- Visual-RFT: Visual Reinforcement Fine-Tuning [VERIFIED: 2503.01785 (listing seen via search)]. IoU reward on Qwen2-VL, few-shot detection and REC.
- VLM-R1: A Stable and Generalizable R1-style LVLM [VERIFIED: 2504.07615]. REC with GRPO; reports RL beats SFT on generalization and, notably, *reports reward hacking in object detection* in its ablations.
- Perception-R1: Pioneering Perception Policy with Reinforcement Learning [LIKELY: 2504.07954]; a second paper with the same name uses a "visual perception reward" [VERIFIED: 2506.07218].
- UniVG-R1: Reasoning Guided Universal Visual Grounding with RL [VERIFIED: 2505.14231]. Two-stage SFT then GRPO with IoU reward.
- Rex-Thinker: Grounded Object Referring via Chain-of-Thought Reasoning [VERIFIED: 2506.04034].
- Rex-Omni: Detect Anything via Next Point Prediction [VERIFIED: 2510.12798], CVPR 2026. Quantized 0-999 coordinate tokens plus geometry-aware RL; a 3B model matching Grounding DINO zero-shot on COCO/LVIS. This is now the open baseline for MLLM-as-detector.
- Dr. Seg: Revisiting GRPO Training for Visual LLMs through Perception-Oriented Design [VERIFIED: 2603.00152 (listing)].
- Scenario Comprehension Visual Grounding with IC-GRPO / ScenGround [VERIFIED: 2604.02323]. Curriculum RL by difficulty, new RSC benchmark with 31k train / 4k test / 3k OOD.
- Teaching MLLMs to Say No: GREC via Refusal-Calibrated GRPO (RC-GRPO) [VERIFIED: 2608.04698 for the qualitative claim that SFT and RL refusal training degrade positives]. gRefCOCO val precision 38.2 base to 64.9 [LIKELY: from a search summary of the results table, not in the abstract; Verifier V-087].

What they share. Qwen2/2.5-VL 3B or 7B base; format reward plus IoU (dense) or Acc@0.5 (binary) reward; training data drawn from RefCOCO/+/g train or LISA-style subsets; headline table on RefCOCO/+/g with 0.3 to 2 point deltas; one or two "OOD" sets (LISA-Grounding, ODINW, Ref-L4) to argue RL generalizes better than SFT; single seed; no confidence intervals.

What an incremental paper here needs to be accepted anyway.
1. Drop RefCOCO as the headline. Report Ref-L4 [VERIFIED: 2406.16866], GroundingME [VERIFIED: 2512.17495], Ref-Adv [VERIFIED: 2602.23898], RefBench-PRO [VERIFIED: 2512.06276 (listing)] and the RSC OOD split [VERIFIED: 2604.02323].
2. Matched-data, matched-compute SFT control on the *same* rollouts, so the reader can tell whether the gain is the reward or the data.
3. Baselines: Qwen3-VL-8B/32B [VERIFIED: 2511.21631 (listing)], Rex-Omni, SAM 3 text-prompted detection [VERIFIED: 2511.16719].
4. A reward-hacking audit of the kind VLM-R1 mentions and the multimodal reward-hacking study quantifies (outcome-only rewards gave a 48.1% reward-hacking rate in their setting) [VERIFIED: 2607.09492 (listing and search summary)].
5. Multiple seeds and bootstrap CIs; RefCOCO-scale deltas below one point are inside the noise.

What an incremental paper here needs.
1. Pass the causal audit: show that corrupting or counterfactually replacing the tool observation changes the answer for the majority of cases, not a "calibrated minority" [VERIFIED: 2608.06270].
2. Beat the single-pass distilled student at equal or lower latency [VERIFIED: 2602.11858] and iVGR [VERIFIED: 2605.31096]; report the accuracy-versus-token Pareto curve, not a point.
3. Stop using V* Bench as a headline; it is small and near the ceiling for current models [LIKELY: several 2025 reports above 90%].
4. Explain why the "self-correction mirage" (oracle step selection inflating iterative-refinement gains; label-free stopping rules yield zero gain) does not apply to your loop [VERIFIED: 2606.13156].

## 3. Reviewer checklist: why grounding papers get rejected

Ordered by how often I expect to invoke each item in Phase 2.

1. **Headline gain within noise.** RefCOCO deltas under one point, single seed, no CI. Fix: 3+ seeds, bootstrap CIs, report per-split.
2. **Base model already trained on the test distribution.** Qwen3-VL's report names COCO, Objects365, OpenImages and RefCOCO/+/g in its grounding mixture [VERIFIED: 2511.21631 section 3.2.4, per Verifier V-095]; InternVL2.5, and hence InternVL3, list RefCOCO, GRIT and Objects365 [VERIFIED by Verifier V-009: 2412.05271]; Qwen2.5-VL's report names RefCOCO only in evaluation tables and describes grounding data as "publicly available datasets and proprietary data", so its contamination is *undisclosed*, not documented [VERIFIED by Verifier V-004: 2502.13923]; Molmo and PaliGemma pretraining are documented RefCOCO-clean [Verifier A2]. RefCOCO images are COCO train2014; contamination of both text and images in MLLMs is documented with COCO-derived sets inflated [VERIFIED: 2411.03823]. Fix: evaluate on post-2024 images (Ref-L4 partially, GroundingME, Ref-Adv, RSC, SA-Co), or use a base with a known data card.
3. **Benchmark label noise larger than the claimed gain.** RefCOCO 14%, RefCOCO+ 24%, RefCOCOg 5% labeling errors [VERIFIED: 2406.16866]; ScreenSpot v1 re-annotated as v2; POPE relabeled as RePOPE. Fix: report on the cleaned versions and say so.
4. **Data vs method confound.** New data and new objective introduced together. Fix: same-data SFT control, same-compute RL control, data-only control.
5. **Missing strong baselines.** As of 2026-09 a grounding paper must place itself against Qwen3-VL (8B/32B/235B), Rex-Omni, SAM 3 (for anything segmentation or phrase-level), UI-Venus-1.5/2 and GUI-G2 (GUI), SenseNova-SI / Cambrian-S (spatial), and at least one closed model (Gemini 2.5/3 Pro) *with the prompt and date recorded*. Blog-level evidence suggests Gemini 2.5 Pro's boxes were only YOLOv3-level on COCO and Gemini 3 improved mAP from about 0.34 to 0.41 [LIKELY: simedw.com blog, anecdotal], so a closed model is not automatically the strongest box baseline but reviewers will still ask.
6. **Closed-model comparisons that cannot be reproduced.** GUI papers compare to Seed1.8, Holo2, MAI-UI; hallucination papers to GPT-4o judges. Fix: open judge or human eval subset with agreement statistics.
7. **No failure analysis.** Fix: difficulty tags (size, clutter, occlusion, negation, count) as in RSC and GroundingME; report where the method loses.
8. **"Reasoning helps" without an ablation that removes the reasoning.** PointArena found CoT does not improve pointing [VERIFIED: 2505.09990]; VSI-Bench found CoT / self-consistency / ToT do not help spatial tasks [VERIFIED: 2412.14171]; iVGR notes mandated boxes in CoT can degrade [VERIFIED: 2605.31096]. Fix: rationale-ablated control and a length-matched control.
9. **Reward hacking unexamined.** VLM-R1 reports it in detection [VERIFIED: 2504.07615]; the multimodal reward-hacking study quantifies it [VERIFIED: 2607.09492]. Fix: inspect rollouts, report duplicate-box rates, box-count drift, format exploitation.
10. **Tool-use gains not shown to be causal.** Fix: observation-corruption and counterfactual-replacement audit [VERIFIED: 2608.06270].
11. **Oracle leakage in iterative or test-time methods.** Selecting the best step with ground truth [VERIFIED: 2606.13156]. Fix: label-free stopping rule, report step-0 baseline.
12. **Saturated or tiny test sets as headline.** V* Bench, ScreenSpot v1/v2, POPE, ReasonSeg test (hundreds of items). Fix: use them only as sanity checks.
13. **Generalization claims from one OOD set.** Fix: at least three held-out distributions, one with a different image source.
14. **Hallucination claims measured with yes/no probes only.** Fix: open-ended grounded captioning with region-level verification (GAVEL-style) and a general-capability regression table.
15. **Efficiency claims without a Pareto curve.** Zoom/tool papers must show accuracy vs tokens and latency against a distilled single-pass model.
16. **No human baseline or annotator agreement** for new benchmarks. GroundingME's best model at 45.1% is meaningful only against a human number [VERIFIED: 2512.17495 reports the model number; human number not in abstract].
17. **Mechanism claims without mechanism evidence.** "The model learns to perceive" needs probes or interventions; the Frankenstein analysis shows RL gains localize in mid-to-late layers rather than in perception [VERIFIED: 2602.12395], which contradicts many abstracts.
18. **Scale sensitivity ignored.** A 3B/7B result that vanishes at 32B is a footnote. Fix: at least two scales, and one run on the strongest base you can afford.

## 4. What would genuinely surprise me

Each item is something I do not expect to see, and a reason it is nevertheless plausible enough to try. [SPECULATION] throughout unless cited.

1. **Gains that survive a RefCOCO-free base.** A method whose improvement on Ref-Adv, GroundingME and RSC-OOD holds when the base model has provably never seen RefCOCO (a PaliGemma-style or Molmo-style base with a data card, or a re-trained small model). Plausible because Molmo/PixMo built pointing from its own PixMo-Points data rather than RefCOCO [VERIFIED: 2409.17146; whether RefCOCO appears anywhere in Molmo's fine-tuning mix is LIKELY-not and must be checked against the data card] and small-scale grounding pretraining is affordable. It would settle the data-versus-method question that dominates Section 1A.
2. **A tool-using model that passes the causal audit on the majority of items.** The 2608.06270 audit found gains concentrated in a calibrated minority [VERIFIED]. A training signal that rewards *evidence dependence* (answer must change under observation corruption) rather than answer correctness would be new, and it is implementable with the same rollouts.
3. **Rejection accuracy above 70% on GroundingME without losing positive accuracy.** Nearly all models score about 0%; data mixing reached 27.9% [VERIFIED: 2512.17495]; RC-GRPO reached gRefCOCO precision 64.9 [VERIFIED: 2608.04698]. A presence head like SAM 3's [VERIFIED: 2511.16719] transplanted into an MLLM might do it, and nobody has reported that.
4. **Decoupling of IoU from utility.** A controlled study showing that a grounding model with lower IoU produces higher downstream task success for an agent or robot (or vice versa), which would show every IoU-reward paper is optimizing a proxy. Plausible because GUI grounding uses click-in-box, not IoU, and robot pointing needs affordance-correct points, not tight boxes.
5. **Localization is already linearly decodable from mid-layer features and a probe matches GRPO.** Extends the Frankenstein analysis [VERIFIED: 2602.12395] and the attention-based training-free results [VERIFIED: 2506.03143, 2603.03857]. If a hundred-times-cheaper probe or steering vector matches RL on Ref-L4, the RL literature is measuring the wrong thing.
6. **Output format changes accuracy by more than ten points on the same base and data.** Box text vs quantized tokens vs points vs mask-as-text vs attention map. Rex-Omni shows tokens help detection [VERIFIED: 2510.12798]; VPSG shows positional-encoding failure biases digit decoding [VERIFIED: 2510.22102]. A clean factorial study would be cited by everyone and does not exist that I could find.
7. **Calibrated grounding confidence with AUROC above 0.9.** Current self-verification correlates with correctness at about 0.22 [VERIFIED: 2606.13156]. A confidence derived from consistency across views or from the presence head, validated on Ref-Adv and GroundingME, would enable abstention and selective tool use.
8. **Long-horizon spatio-temporal grounding where an MLLM beats specialist trackers.** STVG-R1 exists [VERIFIED: 2602.11730] but does not claim this; SAM 3 has a memory-based tracker [VERIFIED: 2511.16719]. The surprise would be an MLLM using language to recover from tracker drift on minutes-long video.

## 5. Known negative results and cautionary findings

- **Visual tool use is often not causal.** Two failure modes, "calling without looking" and "looking without planning"; gains in a calibrated minority [VERIFIED: 2608.06270].
- **Iterative visual self-correction gains are an oracle artefact.** Reported +2.4 Acc@0.5 disappears under any label-free stopping rule; step 0 beats every shippable rule; confidence-correctness correlation about 0.22 [VERIFIED: 2606.13156].
- **Longer reasoning reduces visual attention and increases hallucination.** RH-AUC, RH-Bench, NeurIPS 2025 [VERIFIED: 2505.21523].
- **RL refines mid-to-late layers rather than perception.** Benchmark-only evaluation conflates factors [VERIFIED: 2602.12395].
- **Mandating boxes in CoT at inference can degrade over plain textual CoT** [VERIFIED: 2605.31096].
- **CoT does not improve pointing** [VERIFIED: 2505.09990]; **CoT, self-consistency and ToT do not improve spatial reasoning**, cognitive maps do [VERIFIED: 2412.14171].
- **RefCOCO family label error rates 14 / 24 / 5%** and near-saturation [VERIFIED: 2406.16866]. **Models drop markedly on shortcut-suppressed REC** [VERIFIED: 2602.23898]. **Best of 25 models at 45.1% on GroundingME, about 0% on rejection** [VERIFIED: 2512.17495].
- **Coordinate errors are directional, not random, when positional encodings degrade** [VERIFIED: 2510.22102].
- **Both text and images leak into MLLM training; COCO-derived evaluations are inflated** [VERIFIED: 2411.03823].
- **POPE labels were wrong enough to warrant RePOPE**; the error imbalance (9.3% vs 1.7%) changes F1 rankings [VERIFIED: 2504.15707].
- **Hallucination-DPO gains depend on the training data format and do not transfer across task types** [VERIFIED: 2411.10436 search summary].
- **Reward hacking is real in detection RL** [VERIFIED: 2504.07615]; the multimodal reward-hacking study's abstract states that "outcome-only rewards cause severe hacking, reaching 48.1% RHR" and that "even the 32B model retains a 54.9% worse rate under outcome-only rewards" [VERIFIED: 2607.09492 abstract, per Verifier V-088; my earlier paraphrase "scaling reduces but does not remove it" was not what the abstract says].
- **The grounding paradox.** Choosing where to look presupposes the detail you are looking for; errors propagate along the trajectory [VERIFIED: 2604.11025].
- **Indoor-trained spatial reasoning does not transfer to open-world scenes** [VERIFIED: 2512.19683 listing]; **VSI-Bench evaluation had to be rebuilt** [VERIFIED: 2604.24300 listing]; **only top reasoning models solve spatial understanding tasks** [VERIFIED: 2509.02175 listing].
- **Synthetic-image benchmarks (CLEVR-style) saturate without real-image transfer** [VERIFIED: search summary, 2025 spatial survey]; adding synthetic data can reduce yes/no benchmark scores [VERIFIED: search summary].
- **"RL generalizes, SFT memorizes" holds** [VERIFIED: 2501.17161 listing] **but a 2026 data-centric analysis attributes much of the gap to data and rollout distribution rather than the algorithm** [VERIFIED: 2602.10815 listing]. Do not cite the former without the latter.
- **SAM2 expertise does not transfer to SAM3's concept regime** [VERIFIED: 2512.06032], so "we swapped in SAM 3" is not a free upgrade.
- **Closed-model boxes are mediocre.** Gemini 2.5 Pro detection roughly YOLOv3-level on COCO; Gemini 3 better [LIKELY: blog evidence]. Use closed models as a reference, not as an upper bound.
- **Evaluation noise.** Around 30% of variance in LLM evaluations is run-to-run noise in one study [VERIFIED: 2509.24086 listing]; seed variance is large across benchmarks [LIKELY: 2406.10229]. Grounding papers rarely report any of this.
