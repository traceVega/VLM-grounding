# Skeptic critique of IDEA-91: Interventional grounding verification

Idea file: `ideas/IDEA-91-interventional-grounding-verification.md` (Researcher3, author of record; Researcher1 contributes Section 3.6a; Researcher2 the engineering path). Reviewed version: UNDER REVIEW, last updated 2026-09-02, plus Researcher1's contributed section `ideas/IDEA-91-R1-section-edit-server.md`.

## Round 1 (2026-09-02)

**Verdict: MINOR REVISION.** Conditional: the verdict drops to MAJOR REVISION if 91-M4 (predictive-validity design) is not reworked, because contribution 2's headline claim is not testable as written.

Summary judgement. This is the strongest file in the set. The contribution order is right, the direct arm against cheap counterfactuals is pre-registered with a stop rule, natural pairs remove the artifact confound from the evaluation, the expression-support rule fixes the obvious hole in invariance, and every pre-screen objection has a design answer in the Discussion log. What remains: one missing closest-prior (VISE) that anchors alterations on predicted regions; a validation design for the metric whose sample size cannot support its claim; a circularity in how GroundLive expressions are generated and scored; and several definitional gaps in the necessity operator. All are fixable without changing the idea.

### Objections

**91-N1 (NOVELTY, missing closest prior).** VISE trains Qwen3-VL-2B label-free on raw images with a geometric-invariance reward (spatial consistency under known transformations) and a semantic-invariance reward that alters the model's *own predicted regions* and requires it to recognize the missing information, reporting +16.9 CIDEr on COCO captioning and a 5.0-point CHAIR-I reduction across four model families [VERIFIED: 2606.27373 abstract]. That is a prediction-anchored necessity-and-invariance reward, which is the anchor Section 4 claims as the delta. It is not cited. Required: add VISE to the three closest papers (displacing PAPO to the further-deltas list), read its method section to establish whether the alterations are pixel edits, masks or feature perturbations and whether any localization output is evaluated (I have asked the Verifier to check), and state the delta as "localization outputs with box/none/set semantics, control edits, natural-pair validation and predictive validity", not as "prediction-anchored". If VISE's alterations turn out to be realistic edits on predicted regions, arm (b) of the direct comparison should be VISE's reward, not only VIGIL's.

**91-M4 (EVAL, the predictive-validity claim is underpowered as designed).** Contribution 2 rests on "Spearman(IC on the post-cutoff pool, held-out shifted accuracy) across the audited models is at least 0.7 and higher than RefCOCO Acc@0.5's". The audit has about ten models. A Spearman of 0.7 at n = 10 has a 95% CI of roughly 0.1 to 0.9, and a test that one correlation exceeds another at n = 10 has no power. Worse, RefCOCO Acc@0.5 is saturated across the top models (spread 1 to 2 points [VERIFIED by Verifier A4(a)]), so any metric with variance will "beat" it; that comparison is not informative. Required: (a) raise n to at least 30 units by including intermediate GRPO checkpoints, multiple sizes per family, and the training-arm models, or move to an item-level design (does per-item IC predict per-item correctness on held-out labelled sets, AUROC with item-level CIs, which is already what K3 measures); (b) replace RefCOCO as the comparator with Ref-L4 Acc@0.75 or GroundingME accuracy on pre-cutoff images, so the claim is "IC on unlabelled post-cutoff images tracks labelled hard-benchmark accuracy" rather than "IC beats a saturated number"; (c) rewrite K4 accordingly. Without this, a reviewer reads contribution 2 as unsupported.

**91-M5 (METHOD, GroundLive expressions are circular and biased toward easy referents).** Expressions are written by a captioner plus LLM and "verified by the judge on the factual image", and S is scored by the judge. Items are therefore pre-selected to be judge-verifiable, which inflates S by construction, and captioner-derived expressions favour salient, large referents, which is the regime where IoU already works and the opposite of GroundingME's Limited and Discriminative dimensions. Required: expression generation and verification by a model of a different lineage from the S judge; a difficulty target for the pool (match GroundingME's referent-size and instance-count distribution, or SAM 3 instance-count strata); report the S pass rate on GroundLive against the human anchor per stratum. Also state how "post-cutoff" is enforced per model (cutoffs differ) and how near-duplicates of older images are removed (perceptual-hash against the known training sets at minimum), since Creative-Commons uploads are often re-uploads.

**91-M2 (METHOD, what exactly is removed).** `rem_R` removes "a SAM 3 mask refined from the box". When the model's box is loose or wrong, SAM 3 snaps to some object inside it, possibly the correct referent the model did not actually localize, so N is then measured on a region the model did not predict. Required: define the removal region precisely (box-region inpaint versus SAM-refined mask), report the box-to-mask IoU distribution, run K1's artifact gate on whichever variant is used (rectangular holes are more detectable than mask holes), and for boxes containing more than one SAM 3 instance say which is removed or remove the union.

**91-M3 (METHOD, N conflates "moved to the right fallback" with "moved anywhere").** A pass on N is "none" or a new box with IoU below 0.3 to R. When the expression admits a second referent after removal (multi-instance photos, non-unique expressions, which Ref-L4 found in 14% of RefCOCO), the correct output is the next instance, not "none", and N cannot tell that from a random move. For reliance this is acceptable; for the right-box-wrong-reason rate it is not. Add the S relation on the post-removal output as a reported variant (N+: the fallback box must itself satisfy the judge, or the output must be "none" when no instance remains), and use N+ for the headline rate on the validated subset.

**91-A1 (ASSUMPTION, IC inherits SAM 3's failures).** IC is "label-free" but depends on SAM 3 concept instances for `supp(q)`, for the edit bank, and for the distractor insertions. Where SAM 3 misses the referent, an "invariance" edit can land on the referent and V is mis-scored; where SAM 3 merges instances, N removes too much. Report SAM 3 recall on the human-validated subset and IC's sensitivity to `supp(q)` errors (recompute IC with `supp(q)` from a second detector). This also matters for the release: SAM 3's custom licence governs the derived edit bank.

**91-E1 (EVAL, K2 measures editor quality as much as model failure).** A model that emits a box on a removal edit may be responding to editor remnants. K2 should record judge-verified removal success and a 200-item human check of removal quality, and report the "emits box on removal" rate only on human-verified clean removals.

**91-E2 (EVAL, the headline rate must exclude contaminated items).** RefCOCO/+/g val images are COCO train2014 and appear in RefCOCO train with other expressions [LIKELY: dataset construction]; Qwen3-VL and InternVL train on RefCOCO [VERIFIED by Verifier V-004, V-009]. A removal edit on such an item tests memory as much as perception. The file says RefCOCO is "for comparability only"; make explicit that the right-box-wrong-reason headline is computed on GroundingME, OpenRef, Ref-Adv (2026), natural pairs and GroundLive, and report the RefCOCO-family rate separately as a memorization probe, which is itself an interesting row.

**91-F1 (FEASIBILITY, the evaluation edit bank is unbudgeted).** The 20k-image edit bank in Section 7 is the training pool. The audit needs edits on benchmark images: GroundingME (about 1k), Ref-L4 subsample, OpenRef, gRefCOCO no-target, two Ref-Adv sets, at several operators each, plus judge calls, and for tool-use models the anchor is the crop rather than a box, which needs its own removal recipe. Add a line for the evaluation bank (Researcher2's per-edit costs suggest 10 to 20 GPU-h) and define the anchor per model class: box-in-trace (GRIT, TreeVGR, Rex-Thinker), crop (DeepEyes), none available (iVGR at inference, GazeVLM internal tokens), in which case those models are audited on their final box only or dropped from P-A3.

**91-M6 (METHOD, judge inside the reward).** The training reward includes S, so an open VLM judge sits inside GRPO. VLM-as-judge verification reduces hacking relative to keyword checks but does not remove it [VERIFIED: 2607.09492 search summary]. The judge-swap ablation is the right control; add a held-out-judge evaluation (train with judge A, evaluate S with judge B and with humans) so a policy that learned judge A's quirks is caught.

**91-M7 (METHOD, minor).** The interpretation rule for grounded-CoT models says "output unchanged when the box's region is removed means the box was decorative". For those models the output under test should be both the final answer and the emitted box; report both, since a model whose answer changes while its box stays is a different failure from one where neither moves.

### What would raise the verdict to PROMISING
1. Cite VISE and settle the delta against it after reading its method (91-N1).
2. Rework the predictive-validity design to n of at least 30 units or item-level AUROC, with Ref-L4/GroundingME as the comparator and K4 rewritten (91-M4).
3. Break the GroundLive circularity and add the difficulty target (91-M5).
4. Define the removal region and add N+ (91-M2, 91-M3).
5. Budget the evaluation bank and define anchors per model class (91-F1).

### The two objections that matter most
91-M4 (contribution 2's claim cannot be supported by a ten-model correlation against a saturated comparator) and 91-N1 (VISE anchors alterations on predicted regions and must be positioned against). The rest are definitional and are one revision away.

### New objections (analysis design; no change to the idea)

**91-E4 (EVAL, the unit-level sample is not 70 independent units).** Of the n >= 70 units, 60 are intermediate GRPO checkpoints (5 checkpoints x 4 arms x 3 seeds) from the idea's own training runs on one base. Checkpoints along one run are strongly correlated, and arm (c) is trained to maximize IC, so IC on GroundLive rises by construction along those runs. A Spearman with a plain bootstrap over such units overstates the evidence. Required: (i) report the effective sample size and use a cluster bootstrap with clusters = training run (arm x seed) and base lineage; (ii) report the unit-level correlation with and without arm (c) checkpoints, and with and without all training checkpoints (base models and sizes only, about 10 to 12 units, honestly labelled as underpowered); (iii) state the item-level AUROC as the primary evidence for contribution 2, which the file already does, and make the unit-level result explicitly secondary.

**91-E6 (EVAL, show that the interventional relations add value over the judge).** Item-level AUROC of IC for correctness will be driven largely by S, i.e. by an open VLM judging a crop, which is a known capability (verification-based zero-shot REC [VERIFIED: 2509.09958 (listing)]; OpenRef's consistency checker [VERIFIED: 2605.25706]). For contribution 2 to be more than "a VLM judge can verify boxes", report AUROC for S alone, for N+ and V alone, and for IC, on the same items, and show that adding N+ and V raises AUROC or improves calibration on the cases S gets wrong (right box, wrong reason). If IC does not beat S alone, the metric contribution is the judge, and the audit (contribution 1) carries the paper.

**91-E5 (EVAL, minor).** K5's "within 2 points on natural-pair N+ and GroundingME rejection" should be a CI-based rule: drop contribution 3 if the CI of the (c) minus (a/b/b') difference includes 2 points, since GroundingME's rejection split is a few hundred items and 2 points is inside its noise.

**91-M8 (METHOD, minor).** N+ judges S on the post-removal image crop, which may include the inpainted hole when the fallback region is adjacent to R; state that the crop for S under N+ is R' plus supp(q) with the hole masked from the judge's view, or report the judge's flip rate on adjacent fallbacks.

### The two objections that matter most
91-E4 (correlated, self-trained units inflate the unit-level claim) and 91-E6 (IC must be shown to beat the judge alone).
