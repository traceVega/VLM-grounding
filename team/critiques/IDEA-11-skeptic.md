# Skeptic critique of IDEA-11: The latent grounding gap

Idea file: `ideas/IDEA-11-latent-grounding-gap.md` (Researcher1). Reviewed version: UNDER REVIEW, last updated 2026-09-01.

## Round 1 (2026-09-02)

**Verdict: MAJOR REVISION.**

Summary judgement. The audit-and-explanation half (Stages 1 and 2) is the paper and is well designed; it answers my pre-screen conditions (linear-probe arm, MolmoPoint contrast, two-scale kill test, step-0 baselines). The training half (Stage 4) is over-claimed as "label-free", is bounded by a ceiling the author has not computed, and rests on a presence-probe term whose sensitivity is unverified. The related-work section ignores a pre-screen condition: SD-RPN and Reinforced Attention Learning are absent, and the Verifier's anchors surface a 2025 paper that already reports training-free RefCOCO localization from three attention heads. The idea is fixable within one revision, and the fixes mostly shrink it.

### Objections

**11-N1 (NOVELTY, pre-screen condition ignored).** The closest training precedents named in my pre-screen (Section 6.7 of `landscape/skeptic-crowded-map.md`) are not cited: SD-RPN derives pseudo-RoI labels from the MLLM's own mid-layer attention, denoises them without human labels, and trains a single-pass RoI predictor, reporting more than 10% absolute gain on unseen benchmarks from about 10k QA pairs [VERIFIED: 2509.16944]; Reinforced Attention Learning applies policy gradient to attention distributions and adds on-policy attention distillation [VERIFIED: 2602.04884]. Stage 4 must state in one sentence what "frozen read-out teacher for the coordinate decoder" adds over "denoised read-out pseudo-labels for an RoI head" and over "optimize the attention distribution directly". If the answer is "the output is text coordinates instead of an RoI head", a reviewer will ask why that is better, and the idea's own hypothesis (the gap lives in text decoding) argues for the RoI head.

**11-N2 (NOVELTY, audit half partly exists).** "Your LVLM only needs a few attention heads" reports that three localization heads in LLaVA-1.5-13B give RefCOCO 87.2 val / 90.0 testA / 83.3 testB training-free, close to Shikra-13B, and that the average attention map is uninformative, head selection needs about 1,000 labelled pairs, and the method fails on spatial-pooling models and multi-object cases [VERIFIED by Verifier, anchors A4(d): 2503.06287]. That is a read-out-versus-emitted measurement on REC for one model. The audit's delta is therefore "same model, same forward pass, emitted vs read-out, across tasks and scales, stratified", not "first measurement". Cite it, and cite "When Looking Is Not Enough", which shows attention mass on image tokens while the model drifts to a wrong answer [VERIFIED by Verifier: 2605.11559], because it is the counter-evidence to Section 2's "agreement is a confidence signal with a causal grounding".

**11-A1 (ASSUMPTION, "label-free" is not label-free).** R_att needs top-k layer/head selection and a quantile threshold "selected on a held-out split", i.e. labels, and 2503.06287 puts the budget at about 1,000 pairs. R_grad needs the same threshold tuning. So the read-out is a small-label localizer. The honest control is then: train a coordinate head or a GUI-Actor-style attention head on the *same* 1,000 labels and compare to R. If that head matches R, the "latent gap" collapses into "a small supervised probe beats the text decoder", which is IDEA-21's question, and Stage 4 is distillation from a weak supervised model. Report the label budget of every read-out and include the matched-label supervised head as a baseline in Stage 1.

**11-M1 (METHOD, Stage 4 ceiling not computed).** The policy is rewarded for IoU with a frozen teacher's read-out, so its achievable IoU against ground truth is bounded by IoU(R_T, GT). The kill rule accepts the idea if 20% of V-failures have a gap of at least 0.15. At 8B on GroundingME (best model 45.1% [VERIFIED: 2512.17495]) that bounds the label-free gain to roughly 0.2 x 0.55 x (fraction of those the policy actually recovers), i.e. a few points, before reward noise. Write the expected upper bound into Section 6 as a function of the audit's measured gap distribution, and pre-register the point at which Stage 4 is not worth running. The "mixed variant with IoU(V, GT)" is the more likely paper, and it is then a labeled method with a consistency regularizer, which competes with VISTA [VERIFIED: 2606.14579] and PAPO [VERIFIED: 2507.06448] on their terms.

**11-M2 (METHOD, presence-probe necessity term is unvalidated).** The necessity reward and the presence-head labels both rely on the frozen teacher's presence belief dropping when the region is masked. The same base models score near 0% on rejecting ungroundable queries [VERIFIED: 2512.17495], which means their presence belief is largely prior-driven; if p_T(yes) does not move under masking, the term is noise and the presence-head labels are degenerate (almost everything becomes "groundable" or almost everything "ungroundable" depending on delta). Required pre-check, before any RL: on gRefCOCO no-target and GroundingME-Rejection items versus matched positives, report the distribution of p_T(yes | image) minus p_T(yes | masked) and its AUROC for groundability. If AUROC is below about 0.7, drop the term or replace the probe with IDEA-91's edit-pair negatives. Also note that gray-patch masking is the counterfactual DeFacto uses [VERIFIED: 2509.20912] and that VIGIL and PAPO already build training terms from masked or corrupted inputs [VERIFIED: 2606.26387, 2507.06448]; cite them here, not only VISTA.

**11-M3 (METHOD, box conversion and hacking).** Thresholded attention or gradient maps produce blobby regions, so IoU(V, R_T) rewards boxes that cover the blob, and a policy can satisfy it with inflated boxes. Box inflation under IoU rewards is reported for GUI grounding [PARTIALLY per Verifier V-061: 2505.15810]. The sufficiency crop term pushes the other way, but no evidence is given that the two balance. Require: Acc@0.75 and Acc@0.9 on Ref-L4 (not only 0.5), box-area drift relative to the base model, and an ablation without the IoU(V, R_T) term. Separately, R_caus by dropping visual-token blocks puts a Qwen3-VL model with 2D RoPE and window attention out of distribution; masking the pixels (or zeroing attention to the block) is the safer intervention, and the two should be compared once.

**11-M4 (METHOD, multi-instance bias in the audit).** A presence-probe anchor ("is there a {expr}?") attends to every matching instance, so on GroundingME-Discriminative and multi-target items R_att will cover several objects and its IoU with the single GT box is systematically low, while V is forced to pick one. The gap G is then biased against R exactly where the benchmark is hardest, and 2503.06287 reports the multi-object failure explicitly. Report G per GroundingME dimension and exclude, or separately treat, multi-instance items in the headline gap.

**11-E1 (EVAL, confidence comparison is apples to oranges).** The 0.22 figure is a Pearson correlation between a self-verification confidence and correctness inside an iterative loop [VERIFIED: 2606.13156]; the proposed target is an AUROC of 0.80. These are different statistics on different signals. The right comparison set is the uncertainty benchmark for GUI grounding, which evaluates 27 open-weight methods, finds hidden-state and density estimators most stable, and reports conformal-disk shrinkage of 40 to 60% when calibrated [VERIFIED: 2606.25760]. Stage 5 must include at least the best logit-based, sampling-based and hidden-state families from that benchmark as baselines, and report AURC on the same items. The "inspect" action has no evaluation protocol in the file; either define its cost-benefit metric or drop it.

**11-E2 (EVAL, contamination plan is inconsistent).** Qwen3-VL's report names COCO, Objects365, OpenImages and RefCOCO/+/g in its grounding mixture [VERIFIED by Verifier V-095: 2511.21631]. The idea trains probes and RL on Objects365 and OpenImages and calls them clean pools; they are in the base model's pretraining. Ref-L4 is built on RefCOCO-family and Objects365 images [LIKELY: from the Ref-L4 paper's construction, Verifier to confirm]. So for Qwen3-VL every headline benchmark except possibly GroundingME and Ref-Adv shares images with pretraining, and the probes are trained on seen images. State this in the paper; make Molmo2 (documented RefCOCO-free lineage per Verifier A2, pending its data card) the primary base for the clean-image claims, not the secondary.

**11-I1 (IMPACT, the "RL measures decoder training" claim needs the right comparison).** The RL-vs-SFT evidence is 1 to 2 points in-domain and about 8 points OOD on LISA-Grounding for VLM-R1 [VERIFIED by Verifier A4(c): 2504.07615]. A probe matching GRPO in-domain proves little because in-domain is saturated and label-noisy. The claim is interesting only if the probe or read-out matches GRPO on the OOD sets (LISA-Grounding, Ref-Adv, GroundingME). Specify OOD as the venue for Stage 2's comparison.

**11-F1 (FEASIBILITY, scope).** Four task families, six backbones, three read-outs, five stages, three seeds per RL run, in 220 to 370 GPU-h and one paper. The temporal arm alone needs TimeLens-Bench plus a video backbone and its own anchor tokens. Cut to: REC and GUI for the audit; REC only for Stages 2, 4, 5; temporal and pointing as an appendix audit if time remains. The paper is stronger narrow.

**11-E3 (EVAL, minor).** For pointing outputs "IoU(V, GT)" is undefined; use point-in-mask as in PointArena [VERIFIED: 2505.09990]. For Molmo2, coordinates are 0-100 scaled [VERIFIED by Verifier A3], so the coordinate-token anchor differs from Qwen3-VL's 0-1000; say how the anchor is chosen per model.

### What would raise the verdict to MINOR REVISION or PROMISING
1. Cite and position against 2509.16944, 2602.04884, 2503.06287, 2605.11559, plus VIGIL/PAPO/DeFacto for the masked-input term (11-N1, 11-N2, 11-M2).
2. Add the matched-label supervised-head baseline and report each read-out's label budget (11-A1).
3. Write the Stage 4 ceiling as a function of the audit result, and the presence-probe pre-check with a numeric gate (11-M1, 11-M2).
4. Narrow scope to REC plus GUI audit and REC-only training (11-F1); report per-dimension gaps with multi-instance handled (11-M4); add Acc@0.75/0.9 and box-area drift (11-M3).
5. Replace the 0.22-vs-0.80 framing with the 2606.25760 baseline families and AURC (11-E1); fix the contamination statement and promote Molmo2 (11-E2).

### The two objections that matter most
11-A1 (the read-out is a small-label localizer, so the matched-label supervised head is the real control) and 11-M1 together with 11-M2 (Stage 4 has an uncomputed ceiling and an unvalidated reward term). If the matched-label head matches R, the paper becomes an audit plus IDEA-21's question; if the presence probe is insensitive, Stage 4 and the presence head lose their reward.

### New objections (all additive; none changes the idea)

**11-M5 (METHOD, the incumbent pseudo-label baseline is missing from Stage 4).** The RL pool now carries SAM 3 instances for every image, which means an external-detector pseudo-label recipe is available at zero extra cost, and that recipe is what Qwen3-VL's grounding data actually uses (Grounding DINO and Qwen2.5-VL synthesis [VERIFIED by Verifier V-006: 2511.21631]). Arm (a) (read-out teacher) and arm (b) (SD-RPN bypass) must be compared with arm (e): SFT or IoU-GRPO on SAM 3 pseudo-boxes for the same expressions at equal data. If (e) matches or beats (a), the read-out is not a useful teacher and the H3 answer is "the decoder can be repaired, but a detector does it cheaper", which is still a result but must be the stated one. Gate 1 should also report E_D[g] with SAM 3 boxes as GT alongside the human-labelled audit sets, since it is already computed on that proxy.

**11-M6 (METHOD, Gate 2 and the w2 term need a control mask).** Gate 2 compares p_T(yes | I) with p_T(yes | pixel-mask(V)). A presence probe can drop for any mask anywhere ("something was hidden"), which would pass Gate 2 without the probe being sensitive to the referent. Add the matched-size control mask on a non-referent region, as IDEA-91's control-edit protocol does: Gate 2 passes only if the drop under mask(V) exceeds the drop under mask(control) by the AUROC bar, and the w2 term is paid only when the same holds per item. The cached mask grid already supports this at no extra cost.

**11-A2 (ASSUMPTION, the cross-model decoder contrast is a weak H1 test).** "MolmoPoint and Rex-Omni show a much smaller gap than Qwen3-VL at matched perception (same probe IoU)" compares models with different encoders, data and scale; a smaller gap in Rex-Omni could come from its 22M-sample SFT [VERIFIED: 2510.12798] rather than its token format. Label this contrast as suggestive, and route the clean within-backbone test to IDEA-21, whose factorial swaps the decoder on one backbone with fixed data; IDEA-21 should include the read-out probe as an arm so H1 gets one controlled test. This is a coordination item, not a redesign.

**11-E4 (EVAL, minor).** The kill statistic "R beats V by at least 0.15 IoU on 20% of V-failures" counts cases where both are wrong (R at 0.3, V at 0.1). Report the rescue rate (R at IoU 0.5 or above where V is below 0.5) as the primary kill statistic and the 0.15-gap rate as secondary.

**11-E5 (EVAL, minor).** Section 1 says a 100M attention head "matches coordinate-generating SOTA on ScreenSpot-Pro" [2506.03143]; that was true in mid-2025 and is now far below single-model results (UI-Venus-2-27B 74.1 [VERIFIED by Verifier V-085]). Say "matched 2025 SOTA".

### The two objections that matter most
11-M5 (without the pseudo-label arm, Stage 4 cannot claim the read-out teacher is worth using) and 11-M6 (without the control mask, Gate 2 can pass for the wrong reason).

What makes this idea worth pursuing, in one paragraph for the lead: it is the only proposal that asks a mechanistic question with three separable answers (decoding artefact, perception limit, trainable decoder), each with a pre-registered test and a cheap kill experiment (about 8 GPU-h), and it absorbs the calibration and selective-grounding work as evaluation rather than as a claim. Its risk is that the audit finds a small gap at 8B and above, in which case the paper is a negative result about the read-out literature; that is still publishable and the authors say so.
