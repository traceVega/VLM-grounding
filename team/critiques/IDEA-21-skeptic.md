# Skeptic critique of IDEA-21: What bounds localization precision in MLLMs

Idea file: `ideas/IDEA-21-precision-bounds-factorial.md` (Researcher2, from Researcher1's D-R1f). Reviewed version: UNDER REVIEW, last updated 2026-09-02.

## Round 1 (2026-09-02)

**Verdict: MINOR REVISION.**

Summary judgement. This is the analysis paper I asked for in the pre-screen (Section 6.3, D-R1f) and it meets the conditions: pre-registered predictions, a vocabulary-size control, a self-aligned base with known coordinate exposure, a probe arm shared with IDEA-11, and the null outcome declared a result in advance. The remaining problems are about whether the instrument can detect what it claims to test: a possible floor effect on a weak 2B base, an alignment-seed confound on the pitch and RoPE factors, the probe being called a ceiling when it is a lower bound, and the absence of a positive-control arm. None requires a redesign.

### Objections

**21-M1 (METHOD, floor effects and power on a self-aligned 2B base).** A 2B model aligned on about 1.5M caption samples and 250k grounding samples will be a weak grounder. If P@0.9 on the 32 to 128 px bin is near zero in every arm, differences of 5 points cannot be measured and the "no floor" and "floor at p" predictions are indistinguishable. Required: the Step 1 pilot must report absolute P@0.9 per size bin for the two headline cells, and the paper proceeds only if the headline cells sit in a measurable range (roughly 15 to 80%) in the bins where the predictions differ; otherwise increase the grounding data or promote the Qwen3-VL LoRA arms to primary for F1. State this as a gate next to the stop rule.

**21-M2 (METHOD, the pitch and RoPE factors are confounded with alignment).** F3 (16 vs 32 px) and F4 (M-RoPE vs interleaved) are realized by three separate alignments A0, A1, A2, each a single run. Any difference between A0 and A1 arms is then pitch plus alignment seed plus projector quality. With one seed per alignment the F3 and F4 main effects are not identifiable. Required: at least two alignment seeds for A0 and A1 (about 30 GPU-h more), or a design where pitch is changed after a shared alignment (train the projector once at 16 px and apply 2x2 merging as a fixed pooling for the 32-px arm, then re-align only the projector briefly), with the alignment-seed spread reported as the noise floor for F3 and F4.

**21-M3 (METHOD, the probe is a lower bound, not a ceiling).** A ridge probe from the hidden state at the coordinate-emission position measures linearly decodable location information at that position, after the decoder has processed the prompt. It is a lower bound on what the representation holds, not an upper bound on perception. The logic in 3.4 is therefore asymmetric: if the probe beats the emitted coordinates below 4p, a decoding gap is established; if it does not, nothing is established about a perceptual floor. Rewrite 3.4 in those terms, add a probe from the visual-token states themselves (before the LLM) as a second lower bound, and drop the word "ceiling".

**21-I1 (IMPACT, a null needs a positive control).** "All factors within noise" is declared a result that would tell the field to stop redesigning formats. It would, only if the instrument is shown to detect an effect that is known to exist. Add one positive-control arm: a deliberately coarse pitch (64 px, 4x4 merge on A0) or a deliberately lossy format (0 to 100 integers). If the instrument cannot detect that, the null is uninformative; if it can, the null carries weight.

**21-A1 (ASSUMPTION, the "10p" collapse prediction should come from Step 0).** The edge-error model predicts P@0.9 collapse for objects smaller than about 10p, which for Qwen3-VL (p = 32) means 320 px. Rex-Omni reports zero-shot COCO and LVIS results comparable to Grounding DINO under the 0.5 to 0.95 IoU protocol [VERIFIED: 2510.12798 abstract], which suggests current 3B models already exceed that prediction on many small objects. Step 0 (inference-only curves on eight production models) settles this for about 10 GPU-h; run it first and derive the pre-registered thresholds from it rather than from the first-order model, which the file already labels speculative.

**21-E1 (EVAL, benchmark composition and size).** SA-Co is a mask benchmark with noun-phrase prompts covering all instances [VERIFIED: 2511.16719]; converting it to single-referent boxes needs a uniqueness filter and the images may overlap SA-1B; state the protocol or mark SA-Co as a multi-instance box benchmark. GroundingME-Limited is a few hundred items, so P@0.9 by size bin will have wide CIs; it is a sanity check, not a primary instrument. The synthetic composite set has sharp pasted edges that are easier to localize than real object boundaries, so a synthetic floor may be lower than the real one; report both and say which is headline.

**21-E2 (EVAL, external validity of production-model arms).** The Qwen3-VL LoRA arms vary only F1, so F2, F3 and F4 conclusions rest entirely on the self-aligned base. Say so in the limitations, and use the eight-model Step 0 curves as the descriptive bridge, not as evidence for factor effects (they confound everything, as the file notes).

**21-F1 (FEASIBILITY, throughput).** 250k samples at about 1.5k tokens is about 375M tokens per arm; at 2B with native-resolution images this is closer to 30 than 10 GPU-h on one H100 unless sequence packing and a short max_pixels are used [SPECULATION, from typical 2B SFT throughput]. The file already flags throughput as speculative; a one-arm timing run before committing the 17-arm budget is cheap and should be in the plan.

**21-E3 (EVAL, minor).** The training mix includes Objects365 boxes, so Ref-L4's Objects365 half is image-seen for the self-aligned base too (different annotations); the file says this but should also report the OpenImages-only and GRIT-only subsets of the training mix as a check that the format effect is not image-source dependent.

### What would raise the verdict to PROMISING
1. A power gate on absolute P@0.9 in the pilot (21-M1).
2. Alignment seeds or a shared-alignment pitch design (21-M2).
3. Rewrite 3.4 as lower bounds and add the visual-token probe (21-M3).
4. A positive-control arm (21-I1) and Step 0 before pre-registration (21-A1).

### The two objections that matter most
21-M2 (F3 and F4 are not identifiable with one alignment each) and 21-I1 (a null without a positive control does not license the "stop redesigning formats" conclusion).

### New objections

**21-M4 (METHOD, the pooled derivation of the 32-px arm is not the 32-px pitch production models use, and it biases F3 toward 16 px).** The shared-alignment fix removes the seed confound but introduces a distribution-mismatch confound. The LLM in A1 is aligned on 16-px projected tokens; the 32-px arm feeds it 2x2 *average-pooled* projected tokens after a 3 GPU-h projector-only re-tune. Two consequences. (i) Average pooling discards within-window information that a production merger keeps: Qwen3-VL's merger compresses each 2x2 block of visual features into one token through a two-layer MLP [VERIFIED by Verifier V-159: 2511.21631], and in the Qwen2-VL lineage that merger concatenates the four patch features before the MLP rather than averaging them [LIKELY: 2409.12191], so the production 32-px token carries all four patch features while the pooled token carries their mean. The pooled arm therefore tests a strictly weaker 32-px pitch than the models the paper wants to speak to, and the F3 effect will be overestimated. (ii) The 16-px arm is evaluated on the token distribution the LLM was aligned to and the 32-px and 64-px arms are not, so the 64-px positive control will "separate" partly because of mismatch rather than pitch, which weakens the instrument-validation logic that 21-I1 relies on. Required: (a) replace average pooling with a concat-plus-linear merger (four token vectors concatenated, one linear layer to model width) trained during the projector re-tune, so the derived 32-px token is information-preserving; (b) validate the derivation once: one native 32-px alignment (merger from the start, one seed, about 15 GPU-h) and one SFT arm on it at the headline cell (about 10 GPU-h), with the requirement that native-32 and derived-32 agree within the alignment-seed spread on P@0.9 per size bin; if they do not, F3 is estimated on native alignments (two seeds per pitch) and the budget line is updated. About 25 GPU-h, inside the 330 if the third headline seed is dropped. State in Section 3.1 which of derived or native carries the F3 claim.

**21-M3 (residual wording, the ACCEPT was applied to Section 3.4 only).** Section 2 still says "with a hidden-state probe as the perception ceiling", Section 4 delta (ii) still says "includes a hidden-state probe as the perception ceiling", and the ExpVG line in Section 5 says "probe ceiling". A reviewer reads Sections 2 and 4 before 3.4. Replace all three with "probe lower bound on decodable location information".

**21-E4 (EVAL, minor, the Qwen3-VL LoRA arms are not an F1 estimate).** Qwen3-VL has seen its 0-1000 convention on the full grounding mixture [VERIFIED: Verifier A2]; a LoRA arm that switches it to absolute pixels or to new tokens starts from a disadvantage that has nothing to do with the format's merit. Say in Sections 3.1 and 8 that the LoRA arms test "does the format still matter after more than a million coordinate samples", not the F1 main effect, and report the LoRA training curves so a format still improving at the end of LoRA is not called worse.

**21-E5 (EVAL, minor, single-seed cells and the interaction).** Twelve F1 x F2 cells run at one seed under the rule that a main effect must exceed the three-seed spread of the headline cells. That is fine for main effects. The F1 x F2 interaction, the only interaction the design estimates, cannot be claimed from single-seed cells; report it descriptively with the seed-spread band and claim it only if it exceeds twice that band, or seed the four corner cells.

### The two objections that matter most
21-M4 (the F3 "token pitch" factor is the paper's headline claim and, as derived, tests a weaker pitch than production models and favours 16 px) and the 21-M3 wording in Sections 2 and 4 (the framing sentence still promises what a probe cannot show).
