## 1. What the team learned about the field (consensus findings)

These are the facts the shortlist rests on. Each was verified at a primary source unless marked otherwise.

**F1. The canonical benchmarks no longer measure anything useful.** RefCOCO/+/g is saturated (top open models 92 to 96 on val/testA, spread 1 to 2 points), mislabelled (14 / 24 / 5 percent label error per Ref-L4, arXiv 2406.16866), and inside the training mixtures of Qwen3-VL and InternVL2.5/3 by their own reports (2511.21631 Section 3.2.4); Qwen2.5-VL does not document it; Molmo and PaliGemma pretraining are RefCOCO-clean (Molmo2 has no RefCOCO annotations but COCO images enter through VQA sets). The discriminative benchmarks are Ref-L4 at IoU 0.75 and 0.9, GroundingME (best of 25 models 45.1; 2512.17495), Ref-Adv 2026 (Qwen2.5-VL-72B falls from 92.7 to 54.1 under shortcut suppression; 2602.23898), OpenRef, ScreenSpot-Pro (about 62 single-pass for the flagship, 73 to 83 with zoom agents) and OSWorld-G. Every debiased split in the field's history (VQA-CP, Charades-CD, Ref-Adv 2020) was trained on within a year of release.

**F2. Six sub-areas are closed to incremental work.** GRPO with IoU rewards for REC on a Qwen2.5-VL base evaluated on RefCOCO (about ten near-identical papers since March 2025); crop-and-zoom "thinking with images" trained with GRPO and evaluated on V* and HR-Bench (a dozen papers, plus two 2026 audits showing the tool calls are often not causal, 2608.06270, and that the gains distil into a single pass, 2602.11858); SAM-decoder referring segmentation on RefCOCO and ReasonSeg (SAM 3 and Qwen3-VL-Seg are now the baselines); GUI-grounding data scaling on ScreenSpot-Pro (industrial data engines moved it from 18.9 to the 70s in 16 months); DPO variants for hallucination on LLaVA-1.5 with POPE; tIoU-reward GRPO for temporal grounding on Charades-STA. Also crowded: new coordinate-token schemes, training-free attention grounding, "harder REC benchmark" papers, spatial-QA synthesis from 3D scans.

**F3. Five widely repeated claims do not hold as stated.** "Grounding training reduces hallucination" is refuted as a general claim (Geigle, Timofte, Glavas 2024, 2406.14492: grounding objectives have little to no effect on open caption hallucination). "RL with IoU rewards beats SFT" holds only at small data or out of distribution, and a data-centric analysis attributes much of the gap to rollout difficulty filtering (2602.10815). "Attention maps localize the referent" holds for a few heads on average and is unreliable per sample (a model can put attention mass on the image while drifting to a wrong answer, 2605.11559). "CLIP cannot localize" is refuted as stated. "RLVR does not forget" is refuted (2510.21978, 2607.04364); no grounding-RL paper reports a general-capability regression table. Also: GroundingME's headline "0 percent rejection" is a non-thinking-mode, literal-null-box result (20 of 25 models at exactly 0; with thinking enabled all show some rejection), which is itself evidence that rejection failures are partly a decoding-format effect.

**F4. The historian's recurring problems are still open, and two of them are now testable.** Since 2018 the field has known that RefCOCO-family accuracy is largely recognition plus bag-of-words (image-only model 71.2 percent top-2 precision, category-only 84.2 percent on RefCOCOg; Cirik et al. 2018, 1805.11818) and since 2020 that attention supervision worked as well with random cues (Shrestha et al. 2020, 2004.05704). The "right for the right reasons" control has now been run on reasoning traces (2510.23482) and tool observations (2608.06270), where cited visual evidence turned out to be largely not load-bearing, but never on localization outputs themselves. Coordinate-token grounders also lost the localization confidence that Era-2 detectors had (IoU heads), and the interactive, abstaining and multi-instance settings remain weak.

**F5. The 2026 evidence base contains an unexplained regularity.** Six independent results show that internal signals of the same forward pass (selected attention heads, entropy gradients, intermediate-layer states, block ablation) localize the target when the emitted coordinates miss it (2502.17422; three heads give training-free RefCOCO 87.2, 2503.06287; +3.5 mIoU from temporal heads, 2605.21954; InnerZoom, 2606.30084; attention-only IoU regressor r above 0.67, 2606.01612; GUI-Actor, 2506.03143). Two papers already train on such read-outs label-free (SD-RPN, 2509.16944; Reinforced Attention Learning, 2602.04884) and five use attention-derived rewards for reasoning, but none measures the gap against emitted coordinates or tests its cause.

**F6. Practical constraints that shaped the shortlist.** RL for grounding is commoditised: a 3B to 8B GRPO run of about 1,000 steps is 30 to 300 GPU-hours on EasyR1 or TRL; the expensive part of any new idea is the reward plumbing (image edits, judges on long outputs), which precomputed banks and lookups solve. Attention read-outs need all-softmax backbones (Qwen3.5 and later use Gated-DeltaNet hybrid layers with no attention matrix), so Qwen3-VL-4B/8B, Molmo2, InternVL3.5 and Gemma4-E4B are the working bases. Image editing inside an RL loop is feasible only as an offline edit bank keyed by SAM 3 instances (20k images, about 25 to 30 GPU-hours). Person-weeks, not GPU-hours, bind a two-person team.

**F7. Where the opportunity is.** The team converged on five open questions with no satisfactory method: (i) whether a predicted region is causally responsible for the output, measured without labels; (ii) how large the latent-versus-verbalized localization gap is and why; (iii) what bounds localization precision at matched data (decoding, token pitch, coordinate format, positional encoding); (iv) whether grounding metrics track downstream agent utility, by error type; (v) whether region attribution causally reduces hallucination, and whether listener-verified discriminative expressions can be generated at scale. The shortlist below is one proposal per question, with (v) split into two conditional entries.

## 2. Ranked shortlist

Status vocabulary: SURVIVING means two Skeptic rounds completed with a final PROMISING verdict (all six live ideas reached it; no dispute went to arbitration); the Verifier's evidence grade is A (all premises verified at primary sources), B (one or more premises only partially supported) or C (a premise rests on a refuted or unverified claim). Budgets are H100 GPU-hours from the idea files as revised by Researcher2.

### Rank 1. IDEA-91: Interventional grounding verification ("Right box, wrong reason")

File: `ideas/IDEA-91-interventional-grounding-verification.md`. Authors: Researcher3 (author of record), Researcher1 (edit server and control-edit protocol). Status: SURVIVING. Evidence grade: A. Skeptic scores: novelty 4, impact 4, feasibility 3, clarity 4; probability of a strong main-track paper 0.35.

**Research problem.** Grounding is scored by IoU against a reference box, which cannot tell a model that found the referent from one that found the only salient object, the largest instance of the head noun, or the location its training data favours. The evidence that this matters is two decades deep (F4), and the 2025 to 2026 controls on reasoning traces and tool use found that cited visual evidence is mostly decorative. No one has run the corresponding control on localization outputs: does the box move, or become "none", when the referent itself is removed? Meanwhile every hard benchmark is finite and will be trained on (F1). Anyone who acts on a predicted box (GUI agents, robot pointing, grounded captioning) and every RL-with-IoU-reward paper, whose rewards inherit whatever priors the reference boxes carry, needs the answer.

**Core insight.** A predicted region is a falsifiable hypothesis, and image editing makes it cheap to falsify. Anchor a realistic edit on the model's own predicted region, pair it with an artifact-matched control edit elsewhere in the same image (the "always-edited" design, so "was this image edited" carries no information), and require the localization output itself, with box / none / set semantics, to respond. Four relations follow: invariance (edits outside the expression's support must not move the box; IoU at least 0.7), necessity and its stricter form N+ (removing the region must yield "none" or a valid fallback confirmed by a judge), sufficiency (a frozen judge confirms the expression from the crop alone), and text-side controls (null, attribute-swapped and head-swapped expressions must change the output). Their mean is a label-free interventional consistency score, IC, computable on any image, including images uploaded after every model's training cutoff. That is the property a contamination-resistant benchmark needs and no existing benchmark has.

**Proposed methodology.** Three contributions in a fixed order.
- *Part A, the audit.* About twelve models per class on the same evaluation edit bank: plain grounders (Qwen3-VL 4B/8B/32B and 235B by API, Molmo2 4B/8B, InternVL3.5, Grounding DINO plus SAM 3 as the specialist reference), box-in-trace reasoners (GRIT, TreeVGR, Rex-Thinker; every box in the trace is anchored), crop models (DeepEyes, Chain-of-Focus; the crop region is anchored and the observation-replacement protocol of 2608.06270 is run as a comparison), and frontier closed models (Gemini 3 Pro, with prompt, version and date recorded). Item sets: GroundingME, OpenRef, Ref-Adv 2026, natural pairs (COCO-Search18 target-present / target-absent photos, video frames where a tracked object exits, real two-instance photos), and GroundLive (below); RefCOCO-family items appear only as a separated memorization-probe row. Outputs: pass rates per relation, IC, and the right-box-wrong-reason rate (the fraction of IoU@0.5-correct predictions that fail N+ or invariance on human-validated items), stratified by referent size, expression type, instance count and model class. Pre-registered predictions: invariance violations concentrate in small referents and clutter; necessity violations correlate with box-prior solvability (a diagnostic arm runs current RL grounders against centre / head-noun / location-histogram priors on iid versus OOD splits, with a decision rule); box-in-trace reasoners have lower N+ than plain grounders at matched IoU.
- *Part B, the metric.* Human validation on 1,000 stratified items with three annotators (targets: kappa at least 0.6 between sufficiency and "box correct"; AUROC at least 0.85 of IC for human "box correct" among IoU-correct items). Predictive validity in two designs: item-level AUROC of IC for correctness at Ref-L4 IoU 0.75 and on GroundingME (target 0.80), decomposed so that IC must beat the judge alone by 0.03 overall or 0.05 on the judge-wrong subset, and must beat a two-seed disagreement predictor by 0.05 (the label-free accuracy-estimation lineage of 2106.13799 is the baseline); and a unit-level Spearman correlation between IC on unlabelled post-cutoff images and labelled hard-benchmark accuracy across at least 70 nominal units (base models, sizes, training checkpoints) under a cluster bootstrap. GroundLive is a refreshable evaluation generator: post-cutoff Creative-Commons images de-duplicated by perceptual hash against COCO, Objects365, OpenImages and SA-1B, with expressions written by one model lineage and judged by another.
- *Part C, the training signal (secondary).* The same relations as a GRPO reward on Qwen3-VL-4B (Molmo2-4B replicate) over a 20k-image COCO-free pool with a precomputed SAM 3 edit bank (LaMa removals, 4B-editor attribute swaps, copy-paste distractor insertions, matched controls; rewards are lookups), with a positive-rate constraint against always-abstaining and a held-out judge lineage against judge gaming. The pre-registered direct arm compares five counterfactual generators at equal compute: gray mask (DeFacto), blur-ghosting with a visibility reward (VISE), attention-masked blind state (VIGIL), corrupted-image KL (PAPO), and realistic edits with control edits (this idea). If any cheap counterfactual is within 2 points (95 percent CI) on natural-pair N+ or GroundingME rejection, the training contribution is dropped and the paper ships as Parts A and B.

**Why novel and important.** The Verifier found no prior instance of image edits anchored on the model's own localization output with the box as the tested object. The closest work: VISE (2606.27373) anchors a Gaussian blur on the predicted box but rewards a visibility judgment, evaluates captions, and has no control edits; DeFacto (2509.20912) and HalluSegBench / RobustSeg (2506.21546) build counterfactuals on pipeline-chosen or ground-truth regions for answers and abstention; PAPO (2507.06448) and VIGIL (2606.26387) are corrupted-input KL and attention-mask training terms; SCCM (2510.23482) and the tool-use audit (2608.06270) intervene on traces and tools, not on localization. The 2018 to 2020 ancestors are cited (RISE deletion and insertion metrics, 1806.07421; Counterfactual Samples Synthesizing, 2003.06576; object transplanting as an invariance test for detectors, 1808.03305; FP-RefCOCO false-premise expressions, 2312.08366). The delta is the target (the localization output with none / set semantics), the control edits, contextual rather than geometric invariance, N+, natural pairs, and demonstrated predictive validity. If the audit's headline number is large, REC evaluation changes and the grounded-chain-of-thought literature needs the correction that Shrestha gave attention supervision in 2020; if Part B holds, grounding gets its first benchmark that does not expire when it is trained on.

**Experimental validation and kill rules.** K1, artifact gate (2 GPU-hours, first): 5k referent-removed versus 5k control-edited images, mask holes and rectangular holes separately, ResNet-18 and ViT-S probes with the referent location hidden; AUROC above 0.6 stops everything downstream until the editor or control design is fixed; a forensic detector and a fine-tuned DINOv2 are reported as the strong adversary. K2 (10 GPU-hours plus a 200-item human check of removal quality): 2k REMOVE plus CONTROL pairs on GroundingME and OpenRef with Qwen3-VL-8B and Molmo2-8B; stops the training half if models already reject on more than 60 percent of removals, or if edits are confusing (box on more than 80 percent of removals and abstention on more than 30 percent of controls), or if same-box re-prediction on removals is under 20 percent. K3, human validation (about 500 USD). K4, predictive validity (inference only) with the stop rules above. K5, the direct arm (300 to 400 GPU-hours), decides whether Part C ships. Three seeds per training arm, bootstrap CIs over items, per-split reporting, a general-capability regression table (MMMU, MMBench, POPE) before and after any RL run.

**Predicted headline** [SPECULATION]: right-box-wrong-reason rate 10 to 25 percent for plain grounders and 25 to 45 percent for box-in-trace reasoners on human-validated items, with edited-versus-natural-pair discrepancy under 5 points; IC item-level AUROC 0.80 to 0.88 against 0.72 to 0.82 for the judge alone; GroundingME rejection from about 0 to 40 to 60 percent under the reward without loss of positive accuracy. If the rate is below 5 percent for every model class, the finding is "boxes are load-bearing" and the paper stands only as a metric paper if K4 passes.

**Compute and data.** Minimum viable (K1 to K4, audit, metric, one training arm): about 220 GPU-hours. Full paper with the direct arm: about 550. Annotation about 700 USD; frontier API rows a few hundred USD. SAM 3's custom licence governs any released edit bank.

**Main risks.** Editor artifacts leak despite the gate; editors fail on small, cluttered referents, which are the hard cases (mitigated by natural pairs and text-side operators, which need no editing, and by reporting edit success by referent size); necessity measures reliance, not correctness (sufficiency and the human validation carry correctness); cheap counterfactuals match (then the paper is Parts A and B); reward hacking through the judge; SAM 3 errors propagate into the expression support; GroundLive salience bias. The most likely reviewer reading is "VISE with boxes" or "DeFacto for boxes"; the first page must say that the two leading contributions train nothing and that VISE and DeFacto are arms of the comparison rather than the thing compared against. The Skeptic's most likely failure: the frontier models' right-box-wrong-reason rate is under 10 percent and the score is carried by the judge, leaving "a judge can verify boxes and models are mostly fine".

### Rank 2. IDEA-11: The latent grounding gap (MLLMs localize better than they say, and why)

File: `ideas/IDEA-11-latent-grounding-gap.md`. Author: Researcher1 (absorbs the selective-grounding and calibration directions). Status: SURVIVING. Evidence grade: B (its starting fact, that attention localizes, is only partially supported, which is what the idea measures). Skeptic scores after the final revision: novelty 3, impact 3, feasibility 5, clarity 4; probability 0.27. Lead's estimate 0.28 (Section 4).

**Research problem.** Coordinate-emitting MLLMs are evaluated as if the coordinates they write were what they know about location. Six results (F5) show internal signals of the same forward pass localize the target when the emitted box misses it; each is single-task and either inference-only or label-trained. Nobody has measured, for the same model and forward pass, emitted versus read-out localization across tasks, scales and object sizes; nobody has tested whether the difference is a decoding artefact (digit tokens, quantization, directional positional-encoding bias, 2510.22102) or a perception limit; and nobody has tested whether the text decoder can be taught to read its own map, versus bypassing it with a separate RoI head as SD-RPN does. The counter-evidence that attention mass and correctness can come apart (2605.11559) is carried as the starting fact.

**Core insight.** An MLLM contains two localization systems, an implicit one (where the visual tokens that drive the answer are, measurable by attention, gradients or ablation) and an explicit one (coordinate generation), and their per-example disagreement is measurable once each read-out's label budget is stated. Three hypotheses are separable by experiment: H1, decoding artefact (the gap shrinks on models with non-text coordinate heads and a linear probe from hidden states matches the read-out); H2, perception limit (read-out, probe and coordinates fail on the same items); H3, the decoder is trainable (RL that rewards agreement with a frozen read-out closes the gap, while an RoI head bypasses it at lower cost). Whether agreement between the two systems is a usable abstention signal is a fourth, testable question.

**Proposed methodology.** Stage 1, audit on all-softmax backbones at two scales (Qwen3-VL-8B and 32B for the audit and probes, with activation patching at 8B; Molmo2-8B; InternVL3.5-8B; Gemma4-E4B; gradient read-outs on one Qwen3.5 hybrid; Qwen3-VL-4B only for the gated training minimum) over REC (Ref-L4 by size bin, GroundingME by dimension, Ref-Adv 2026, OpenRef) and GUI (ScreenSpot-Pro, OSWorld-G, using IDEA-91's operators in screenshot form, which the lead placed in this idea rather than in IDEA-91). Read-outs with stated label budgets: a zero-label entropy-gradient read-out with one fixed box rule; attention and gradient read-outs tuned on about 1,000 pairs (the budget 2503.06287 needed); a zero-label causal read-out by block ablation of the presence-probe log-odds. Controls: a coordinate-regression head and a GUI-Actor-style attention head trained on the same 1,000 labels on frozen features; if they match the read-out, the finding is "a small supervised probe beats the text decoder" and is reported as such. Metrics: IoU of emitted and read-out boxes against ground truth, their gap and agreement, and the rescue rate (read-out at IoU 0.5 or better where the emitted box fails), stratified by size and dimension; multi-instance items scored separately with a set-aware read-out. Stage 2, cause test: layer-wise linear probes trained on SA-1B with SAM 3 instances; decoder contrasts against MolmoPoint and Rex-Omni (suggestive only; the controlled within-backbone test is IDEA-21's decoder factor); activation patching between the base and an IoU-GRPO copy (VLM-R1 recipe) following 2602.12395, with the decisive comparison out of distribution (LISA-Grounding, Ref-Adv, GroundingME): does a mid-layer probe or the zero-label read-out match IoU-GRPO, and does GRPO reduce the gap or raise read-out accuracy. Stage 3, label-free test-time exploitation: read-out-constrained coordinate decoding and agreement-based selection among samples, against ACS-Free and SD-RPN, with step-0 reported and no oracle selection. The core of the paper, after the Skeptic's round-2 change was applied, is Stages 1 and 2 run at two scales (8B and 32B) with activation patching at 8B. Stage 4 is a gated training minimum, run only after the core and only if two gates pass: Gate 1 requires a measured headroom of at least 0.05 IoU on the training pool; Gate 2 requires the presence probe's referent-mask drop minus control-mask drop to reach AUROC 0.7 for groundability. The reward is agreement with a frozen teacher's precomputed read-out plus the control-masked necessity term, a crop-sufficiency term and an explicit anti-inflation penalty. Three arms at 4B, about 100 GPU-hours: (a) label-free, (b) the SD-RPN RoI-head bypass on the same pseudo-labels, (e) the pseudo-label incumbent (SFT and IoU-GRPO on SAM 3 boxes, which is how Qwen3-VL's grounding data is made). The mixed-label arm and the VISTA and PAPO baselines were dropped; H3 (the decoder is trainable) is listed as a conditional contribution. The general-capability regression table is reported as a contribution, since no grounding-RL paper reports one. Stage 5, selective grounding: actions box / multiple / none; signals include coordinate-token likelihood (free baseline), verbalized confidence, sample dispersion, agreement, a presence head, and a Brier-trained confidence; metric AURC and risk-coverage on GroundingME, OpenRef, Ref-Adv and ScreenSpot-Pro against the 27-method uncertainty families of 2606.25760, HyperClick and SafeGround; negative-result variant: does IoU-GRPO degrade calibration relative to the base.

**Why novel and important.** The exploitation-only version is taken (SD-RPN 2509.16944; RAL 2602.04884; attention-derived rewards for reasoning in 2604.13993, 2602.08241, 2607.01707, 2512.14044, VIGIL) and is not claimed. The delta is the first same-model, same-forward-pass audit across tasks and scales with label budgets and a matched-label control; the cause test that separates decoding from perception with probes, decoder contrasts and OOD comparison against IoU-GRPO; and the gated experiment that asks whether the pathway deployed models actually use can be repaired, judged against the bypass and the incumbent. A large decodable gap at 8B and above would mean the RL-for-grounding literature is training the decoder, not perception, which reframes a crowded area; a small gap is a negative result about the read-out literature. Either outcome is a paper under the H1 / H2 / H3 framing.

**Experimental validation and kill rule.** About 8 GPU-hours, decisive within a week: on Qwen3-VL-8B and 32B, all emitted-box failures on GroundingME, OpenRef and the Ref-L4 small bin; stop the idea if at both scales the rescue rate is below 15 percent and the 0.15-IoU-gap rate is below 20 percent. Stop Stage 4 if the matched-label head is within 2 IoU points of the read-out or Gate 1 fails. Stop Stage 5 if agreement does not beat token likelihood on AURC on two of four benchmarks. Predicted [SPECULATION]: rescue rate 20 to 35 percent for the tuned attention read-out and 12 to 25 percent for the zero-label read-out on small-object failures, with the matched-label head at least 5 points below. Three seeds per RL arm, bootstrap CIs, paired comparisons.

**Compute and data.** Core (two-scale audit, probes, patching including the 8B IoU-GRPO copy that activation patching needs, Stage 3, Stage 5, the GUI column): about 160 to 190 GPU-hours, with no training beyond that copy. With the gated Stage 4 minimum: about 260 to 290. Both authors' accountings now agree on these figures. Contamination: Molmo2 is the primary base for clean-image claims (no RefCOCO annotations; COCO image overlap reported), Qwen3-VL the engineering base; probes and the RL pool on SA-1B; RefCOCO in an appendix only.

**Main risks.** The gap is small at 32B (a scale-dependent gap is still a result); the matched-label head matches the read-out (the paper becomes the audit plus the decoder question, handed to IDEA-21); the Stage 4 ceiling is a few points (Gate 1 prevents running it); box inflation to cover blobby read-outs (anti-inflation term, Acc@0.75/0.9, area drift); "a collection of known observations" (pre-empted by the cause test and the stated deltas). The Skeptic's one change, to drop Stage 4 and spend its budget on a two-scale audit with patching, was applied in substance by the author, who kept the gated three-arm minimum as the smallest test of the repair question; the Skeptic accepted that and raised the feasibility score to 5.

### Rank 3. IDEA-22: Which grounding errors cost task success? Parametric error injection inside computer-use agents

File: `ideas/IDEA-22-grounding-utility-decoupling.md`. Author: Researcher2. Status: SURVIVING (Sections 1, 4, 5 and 8 rewritten after the Verifier's refutation of the original framing; the Verifier's final sweep found no competing output-side injection study). Evidence grade: B after the rewrite (C before). Skeptic scores on the rewritten file: novelty 3 (down from 4), impact 4, feasibility 3, clarity 4; probability 0.25 (down from 0.28). The author's own estimate after the rewrite is 0.20 to 0.25; the lead's is 0.25 (Section 4).

**Research problem.** Every RL-for-grounding paper optimizes an offline metric (IoU, hit rate, a Gaussian around the target) and assumes the gain reaches the agent. Fixed-planner grounder ladders now exist and agree: Agent S2 (2504.00906, Fig. 6), MMBench-GUI (2507.19478, Finding 2: task success grows roughly linearly with grounding accuracy, a 2.8x gain from the grounder versus 1.15x from the planner) and GroundCUA (2511.07332, Table 4), plus Jedi's 5.0 to 27.0 OSWorld ladder (2505.13227). None intervenes on the grounder's error distribution, so none can say which grounding property carries the effect. Whether an agent needs IoU or only a hit, where the precision knee is, whether a near-miss and a wrong-element click at the same offline accuracy have the same cost, and whether abstention beats a confident wrong click, are unmeasured; GUI-G1 (2505.15810) shows hit and IoU rewards pull box size in opposite directions, so the answer decides which reward the RL literature should use.

**Core insight.** Task success depends on the grounder's error distribution, not its accuracy number. Because modular agents put the grounder behind a fixed planner, the grounder is an intervention variable: hold the planner, tasks and seeds fixed, use the accessibility-tree oracle as the injection base so the executed error rate is known, and move one property at a time (Gaussian jitter, directional shift, shrink or inflate at constant hit, element confusion at matched hit-rate loss, refusal versus hallucinated click at matched rate, latency, and an empirical-error replay arm sampled from a real grounder's measured error distribution). The response surface of task success against each property, with MMBench-GUI's linear relation as the pre-registered null, tells which offline metric predicts success, where the knee lies, what abstention is worth under three re-plan policies (retry, rephrase, zoom then retry), and whether reward shape matters downstream.

**Proposed methodology.** Environments: OSWorld (369 tasks, parallel Docker or cloud providers) and AndroidWorld (116 tasks on an emulator). Agent: fixed open planner (Qwen3.5-27B or Qwen3-VL-32B on vLLM; the planner sees screenshots only so grounding is load-bearing) plus a grounder; a pinned closed planner on a 100-task subset for external validity, and a pre-registered branch that promotes the closed planner to the primary injection tier if the open tier is planner-bound. A descriptive ladder of eight real grounders spanning about 40 to 75 ScreenSpot-Pro (replicating the published relation on the single-pass protocol) and the injection arms on the oracle base. Metrics: task success over three seeds with paired bootstrap CIs, steps to success, recovery rate per re-plan policy, failure attribution by oracle replay of the failed step; segmented regression locates the knee; readings are in minimum-detectable-effect terms (about 4 to 5 points at three seeds on full OSWorld) with Holm correction; the grounding-sensitive subset is selected on a held-out seed and reported as secondary.

**Why novel and important.** Against Jedi, Agent S2, MMBench-GUI and GroundCUA (ladders, no intervention), GUI-RobustEval (2605.29447, real root-cause errors replayed into live OSWorld at fixed depth, the closest neighbour) and GUI-Perturbed (2604.14262, offline input-side perturbation), the delta is the first parametric, output-side intervention on a grounder's error distribution inside a closed loop with the planner held fixed, the empirical-replay arm, and abstention under three re-plan policies. The Verifier found no parametric injection study. If identity errors and near-misses differ at matched accuracy loss, reward shape matters and the surface is the contribution; if the surface is linear regardless of error type, that is also the answer the RL-for-grounding literature needs.

**Experimental validation and kill rule.** About 60 GPU-hours plus two person-weeks of harness, and about 60 more to repeat it at a second open planner tier (the Skeptic's final condition, so that a flat surface at one tier is not mistaken for a null): a stratified random 200-task OSWorld subset and all AndroidWorld tasks, open planner, three grounders times three seeds, plus jitter at 0, 16 and 32 pixels on the oracle base (600 task-seed pairs per arm, MDE about 5 points); the main arms then run at the tier with measured leverage. Stop if the weakest-versus-strongest paired difference is below the measured MDE on both environments, or if seed-to-seed variance of a fixed configuration exceeds 8 points. ### Rank 4. IDEA-21: What bounds localization precision in MLLMs? A pre-registered matched-data factorial

File: `ideas/IDEA-21-precision-bounds-factorial.md`. Author: Researcher2 (from Researcher1's D-R1f). Status: SURVIVING (Skeptic final PROMISING recorded after the concatenating-merger derivation check was accepted). Evidence grade: B (ExpVG's effect magnitude is only partially supported). Skeptic scores: novelty 3, impact 3, feasibility 3, clarity 4; probability 0.20.

**Research problem.** Five 2025 to 2026 lines each blame a different bottleneck for high-IoU localization: digit-token coordinates (Hi-Token, 2608.03471), token-by-token box decoding (LocateAnything, 2605.27365; PaDT, 2510.01954), positional-encoding failure at high resolution (VPSG, 2510.22102; RULER, 2510.03230), the 28-pixel merged visual token (MolmoPoint, 2603.28069), and supervision rather than architecture (PGT, 2605.23883). They cannot all be the binding constraint. The only controlled study, ExpVG (2508.08066), used LLaVA-1.5 at 336 pixels and RefCOCO at Acc@0.5; Shikra compared numerals with bin tokens in 2023; Qwen reversed its own coordinate convention between Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen3-VL without an ablation. No one has run the factors against each other at matched data on a native-resolution backbone with high-IoU, size-stratified evaluation, which is where models are far from ceiling (Ref-L4 introduced Acc@0.75 and 0.9 for this reason).

**Core insight.** Precision should obey a measurable floor: if the decoder cannot interpolate within a visual token, per-edge error is bounded below by the effective token pitch (32 pixels for Qwen3-VL, 28 for Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL, 14 to 16 unmerged), and each bottleneck predicts a different P@0.9-versus-object-size curve: quantization predicts no visible floor, pitch predicts collapse below a multiple of the pitch, positional-encoding failure predicts directional errors growing with distance from the origin, and decoding predicts that a probe read from the same hidden states beats the emitted coordinates and that a parallel or token-selection decoder closes the gap at the same perception. A fractional factorial with fixed data, pre-registered predictions and probes as lower bounds on decodable location tests these predictions against each other; "all factors within noise" is declared a result in advance, because it would refute the motivating claims of four papers.

**Proposed methodology.** A self-aligned 2B backbone (SigLIP2-so400m/16 encoder, Qwen3-1.7B text LLM, LLaVA-OneVision-style alignment on captions with no boxes) so the amount of coordinate data ever seen is known. Factors: F1 coordinate format (absolute pixels; 0 to 1000 integers; 0 to 999 special tokens; axis-specific hierarchical tokens), F2 decoder (autoregressive text; parallel box head; token selection over visual tokens with a sub-patch offset; heads matched at about 100M parameters), F3 token pitch (16 pixels from the shared alignment; 32 and 64 by a concatenating merger plus projector re-tune, with one native 32-pixel alignment as a derivation check), F4 position encoding (2D M-RoPE versus interleaved axis-symmetric, two alignment seeds each, with the between-seed spread as the registered noise floor). Vocabulary-size control arm; positive controls (64-pixel pitch and a lossy 0 to 100 format) that the instrument must separate from the main cells by more than the seed spread. About 23 SFT runs on an identical 250k-sample COCO-free mix (Objects365 and OpenImages boxes with captioner-written expressions, a GRIT subset, GUI samples) through one round-trip-tested converter. Evaluation: Ref-L4 at Acc@0.5 / 0.75 / 0.9 by size with its COCO and Objects365 halves separated; SA-Co single-referent subset (mask-derived boxes on non-COCO images); a 5k synthetic composited set with exact boxes at 8 to 512 pixels as the zero-noise floor probe; ScreenSpot-Pro; a 300-item re-annotation to estimate the human-agreement ceiling on P@0.9. Probe arm from the same forward pass on every trained arm: a ridge probe from projected visual tokens (pre-LLM), a mid-layer hidden-state probe, the zero-label entropy-gradient read-out, and the attention-mass box, all scored on the same size bins; this is the controlled within-backbone test of IDEA-11's decoding hypothesis. Inference-only Step 0 curves for eight production models fix the registered thresholds before any arm is trained; Qwen3-VL LoRA arms vary F1 only, and an optional heads-only replicate of F2 on Qwen3-VL-8B (about 40 GPU-hours) is the production-scale check.

**Why novel and important.** Against ExpVG, LocateAnything, Hi-Token, Shikra Section 6.2 and the 2024 small-object factor study (2402.07384), the Verifier found no matched-data factorial. The delta is matched data and compute at native resolution, four factors crossed with positive controls and seed noise floors, size-stratified P@0.9, probes as lower bounds, and pre-registration. One factor dominating by more than 5 points at P@0.9 would be cited by everyone building a grounder; a null within noise tells the field to stop redesigning coordinate formats. The Skeptic's list of results that would genuinely surprise a reviewer includes exactly this factorial.

**Experimental validation and kill rule.** Step 0 (about 10 GPU-hours), a one-arm timing run (10 to 20) that selects the primary or fallback design, and a two-arm pilot (about 20) with a power gate (headline cells' P@0.9 must lie between about 15 and 80 percent in the discriminating size bins). Stop if both hold: production models with different nominal pitch, matched at P@0.5, differ by under 3 points at P@0.9 in every size bin; and the pilot arms differ by under 2 points at every threshold with every probe-minus-emitted gap under 0.03 IoU. Predicted [SPECULATION, replaced by Step-0-derived registered values]: decoder main effect 5 to 10 points P@0.9 on 32 to 128 pixel objects; probe-minus-emitted gap 0.05 to 0.10 IoU for text decoders below four pitches; format 2 to 5 points at P@0.9 for hierarchical tokens; pitch effect present at matched pixels and absent at matched tokens.

**Compute and data.** About 365 GPU-hours in the primary design (24 arms; the float coordinate level restored as one run), which exceeds the team's 300 GPU-hour cap per idea, so fallback design B (13 runs, about 325) is the expected cut; 150 USD re-annotation; the SA-Co licence terms remain unverifiable behind a manual gate, so its use as a released artifact is unresolved; 4 to 5 person-weeks (alignment pipeline, converters, three decoder heads, probes).

**Main risks.** "Engineering, not science" (answered by pre-registration and the probe-based separation of decoding from perception); the self-aligned 2B base is too weak to show differences at P@0.9 or its findings do not transfer (the Skeptic's most likely failure; mitigated by Step 0, the Qwen3-VL arms and the optional 8B decoder replicate); converter bugs, the field's recurring failure; unestimated interactions beyond F1 x F2; label noise capping measurable P@0.9.

**IDEA-92: Does localization cause faithfulness? Attributed description with necessary and sufficient evidence.** File: `ideas/IDEA-92-attributed-description.md`. Authors: Researcher1 (record), Researcher3 (formalism and causal design). Status: SURVIVING as a conditional entry (Skeptic final PROMISING after all three round-2 acceptances were applied: an explicit region-free reward for arm B, a global-label gate with a uniform claim subsample against reward hacking, and a human audit on arms A, B and E with a third-lineage outcome judge as the primary causal outcome; conditional on Gates 0a to 0c and IDEA-91's artifact gate running before the seed SFT). Evidence grade: B. Skeptic scores: novelty 3, impact 3, feasibility 2, clarity 5; probability 0.18.
- *Problem and insight.* Geigle 2024 found grounding objectives have little to no effect on caption hallucination (F3). Grounded captioning (GLaMM, GROUNDHOG) scores phrase boxes by overlap and never asks whether the cited region supports the claim. The sharper question: when a model must cite a region for every typed claim (entity, attribute, count, relation) and is rewarded only if the region is sufficient (a frozen judge entails the claim from the crop, above its text-only prior) and necessary (removing the region, or all duplicate instances, breaks entailment while a matched control edit does not), does hallucination fall relative to the same judge verifying the same claims without regions? A four-outcome attribution metric (attributed with a redundant flag, unsupported, hallucinated, unresolved) audits current models for post-rationalized citations.
- *Method.* Qwen3-VL-4B; seed SFT on auto-attributed DenseWorld-1M; LLM claim decomposition; SAM 3 regions; two judge lineages; IDEA-91's shared edit bank and artifact gate; arms A (attribution reward), B (region-free claim-verification GRPO with the same judge and claims, the control that isolates localization), B' (RLHF-V-style text correction), C (sufficiency only), D and E; evaluation on DetailVerifyBench span rate, RePOPE and DASH-B, human-audited attributed precision, general-capability regression; a user study of the typed-claim interface.
- *Novelty.* Against KAWHI (2603.27375, region credit inside GRPO), COPO (2508.04182, token-level necessity and sufficiency), ClaimDiff-RL (2605.20278), CuRe (2607.05150), ViGoR (2402.06118), GROUNDHOG, FaithScore and Woodpecker: none tests necessity of the cited region, and none isolates attribution from claim verification at equal data.
- *Validation.* Gate 0a: judge AUROC against DetailVerifyBench human spans (5 GPU-hours; below 0.75 on attributes and relations restricts the metric to entities and counts). Gate 0b: a 300-claim human-labelled region-attribution set with per-type kappa gates. Gate 0c: at least 15 percent unsupported-or-redundant citations in current models, else the paper is the causal experiment alone. The frontier four-class audit (under 20 GPU-hours) is worth running regardless. Minimum viable about 120 GPU-hours (causal experiment at reduced steps); about 300 trimmed; 5 to 6 person-weeks.
- *Risks.* The crop judge is unreliable on attributes and relations; the causal experiment replicates Geigle's null, leaving "FaithScore with regions"; long-output RL cost; judge gaming; highest execution risk in the set. The Skeptic's one change: make the frontier audit with human validation the headline and let the causal experiment be the second contribution.

**IDEA-13: Referring games at MLLM scale, verifier-checked self-play for discriminative grounding.** File: `ideas/IDEA-13-referring-games.md`. Author: Researcher1. Status: SURVIVING as a conditional entry (Skeptic final PROMISING after the round-2 acceptances were applied, including the Verifier's finding that GroundingSuite already filters its expressions with a segmenter listener at IoU 0.5, which refuted the "incumbents use no listener" delta and narrowed the claim to distractor-set verification, the RL speaker and the curriculum; the incumbent baseline now carries that filter; the kill experiment decides whether it proceeds). Evidence grade: B. Skeptic scores: novelty 2, impact 3, feasibility 4, clarity 4; probability 0.15.
- *Problem and insight.* Models fail where language must be informative (GroundingME-Discriminative 69.6 for the best model; Ref-Adv 2026), human referring data are under-informative relative to hard same-category distractors, and the VLM-generated expression engines (16M expressions, 2407.14563; GroundingSuite, 2503.10596) never check that a listener can resolve what they write. Informativeness is verifiable by a listener: a speaker is trained with GRPO over an unlabeled pool with SAM 3 same-category distractor sets (similarity-tiered, non-extremal targets to block location shortcuts), paid when a frozen 32B verifier localizes the target uniquely and paid extra when the current student listener cannot (so hard positives enter training), with a rejection mode that has objective ground truth; the listener is trained on the verified corpus.
- *Baselines that decide the paper.* B1, a strong MLLM prompted once with the target and distractor boxes to write a discriminative expression, with the same hard-positive selection; B3, the VLM-engine incumbent at equal size; B7, listener self-training; B8, difficulty-matched SFT. Per-round human-listener success, a held-out-lineage listener, and descriptor-type composition with a location-word removal ablation control drift and shortcuts.
- *Novelty.* Against the 2017 speaker-listener-reinforcer (1612.09542), VisPlay (2511.15661), Vision-Zero and GREx, the delta is the geometric, listener-verifiable win condition, the distractor curriculum and verifier-independent acceptance; the Skeptic rates it thin, and unless the student exceeds its 32B verifier the loop is distillation with distractor conditioning.
- *Validation.* Kill experiment 35 to 45 GPU-hours, run before any other spend on the idea: stop if the game listener gains under 2 points over the better of B1 and B3 (both with the same hard-positive selection) with overlapping CIs on Ref-Adv 2026 (primary, paired MDE) plus GroundingME-Discriminative, if B7 matches the game, or if human-listener success falls under 70 percent; "beats B3 but not B1" also stops it. A pre-registered verifier-exceedance test with a frozen-4B true-self-play configuration decides whether the result is more than distillation. About 225 GPU-hours full; 3 to 4 person-weeks.
- *Risks.* Prompted generation matches the game (the author's own and the Skeptic's most probable outcome); the student never exceeds its verifier; language drift; location shortcuts. The author ranks it their weakest idea.
