## 0. What the paper is, and what changed in this revision
Not a reward paper. Claim-level verified rewards exist (ClaimDiff-RL, CuRe, rubric rewards for long-form hallucination RL), region-attributed credit inside GRPO exists (KAWHI), token-level sufficiency and necessity rewards exist (COPO) `[VERIFIED by Verifier: 2605.20278, 2607.05150, 2603.27375, 2508.04182, 2608.12337 (V-196)]`, decomposition-plus-judge evaluation exists (FaithScore), verifier-ensemble correction exists (Woodpecker), fine-grained reward modelling for the grounding of LVLM descriptions exists (ViGoR), and grounded description with entity masks that claims reduced hallucination exists (GROUNDHOG) `[VERIFIED by Verifier: 2311.01477, 2310.16045, 2402.06118, 2402.16846]`. The paper is (i) a per-claim attribution metric with four outcomes (attributed with an optional redundant flag, unsupported, hallucinated, unresolved), a text-only prior baseline and control edits; (ii) the controlled experiment that isolates whether region attribution causes less hallucination, with the optimizer and judge held fixed (attribution reward versus region-free claim-verification GRPO with the same judge on the same atomic claims, at equal steps and judge calls); and (iii) a user-facing typed-claim interface whose value is measured downstream. The training run exists to serve (ii).

## 1. Research problem

The field assumes that grounding reduces hallucination. The Verifier's anchor A4(b) rates that claim REFUTED as a general claim: Geigle, Timofte and Glavas (2024) added two grounding objectives (referring expressions and grounded captioning) to LVLM training on three backbones and measured POPE, CHAIR and FaithScore; grounding objectives had "little to no effect on object hallucination, both in QA-based evaluation and open-ended captioning" `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-048, V-148: 2406.14492]`. The same paper found that generating grounded captions at inference slightly reduces object hallucination at a slight cost in detail `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-148]`, which is the closest existing data point for this idea's typed-claim output format and the reason arm D (SFT on attributed data) and the base are both in the causal experiment. Meanwhile long descriptions hallucinate at the span level (DetailVerifyBench: token-level annotation of multiple hallucination types in captions over 200 words `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-138: 2604.05623]`), RL for reasoning can raise hallucination `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-139: 2601.06224]`, and grounded description stopped at entity masks scored by overlap: GLaMM `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-142: 2311.03356]` and GROUNDHOG, which retrieves entity masks from holistic segmentation and claims reduced object hallucination `[VERIFIED by Verifier: 2402.16846]`, never ask whether a cited region supports its claim. GAVEL shows closed models do poorly at verifying, explaining and localizing caption-image misalignment `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-140: 2606.26923]`.

So the open question is sharper than "does grounding help": grounding *data* does not reduce caption hallucination (Geigle); does an attribution objective that enforces necessity and sufficiency of the cited region do so, at equal data, when the only thing that differs from a region-free verified-claim reward is the region? Why it could succeed where grounding objectives did not: Geigle's objectives ask the model to emit boxes, not to have its claims checked against them; the attribution reward pays only for claims whose cited region entails the claim and whose removal breaks it, so the model is trained on the dependency between text and evidence rather than on the co-occurrence of text and boxes. Matching Geigle's null result is the pre-registered failure outcome.

Who cares: hallucination researchers (a causal answer to a decade-old assumption), dense-captioning data engines, and users who need verifiable descriptions (accessibility, medical and document reporting), for whom "click the claim, see the evidence" is the interface.

## 2. Core insight

Attribution converts hallucination detection into grounding verification, but only if attribution is defined causally. A cited region must be sufficient (a frozen judge entails the claim from the crop alone, above the judge's text-only prior) and necessary (the judge no longer entails the claim after the region, or the set of matching instances when the object has duplicates, is removed, while a matched control edit elsewhere leaves it entailed). With that definition each claim falls into one of four classes, attributed (optionally flagged redundant), unsupported, hallucinated or unresolved, which is a measurement no existing grounded-caption metric makes. Training with a reward built on this definition and comparing it against a region-free reward from the same judge on the same claims isolates the causal role of localization.

## 3. Proposed methodology

### 3.1 Shared notation (copied in full from Researcher3, landscape/r3-history.md Section 7, revision of 2026-09-02, so IDEA-91 and IDEA-92 use one formalism)
- Inputs. Image `I`; query `q`, which is a referring expression (IDEA-91) or an atomic claim `c` extracted from a description (IDEA-92); model output `y = f(I, q)` with `y` a box / mask / point set, the token `none`, or a set of boxes. For descriptions the model also emits a cited region `R_c` per claim.
- Edit operators on a region `R`. `rem_R(I)`: remove the content of `R` and inpaint. `ctrl_R'(I)`: artifact-matched control edit on a non-overlapping region `R'` of the same size and shape. `crop_R(I)`: crop to `R` with a small margin, upsampled to the judge's native resolution. `move_R(I)`: relocate the content of `R`. `ins_R''(I)`: insert a look-alike distractor at `R''`.
- Judges. `J(I, q)` in `[0, 1]`: a frozen verifier's probability that `q` is satisfied in `I`. `J_text(q)`: the same verifier with no image (the text-only prior).
- Necessity `N(R; I, q)`: `f(rem_R(I), q) = none` or `IoU(f(rem_R(I), q), R) < t_low`, and `IoU(f(ctrl_R'(I), q), y) >= t_high`. The second clause is what separates "responds to the referent" from "responds to editing".
- Sufficiency `S(R; I, q)`: `J(crop_R(I), q) >= theta`, and for claims the prior-adjusted form `J(crop_R(I), q) - J_text(q) >= delta`, so a claim that is true of most images does not count as grounded.
- Invariance `V(R; I, q)`: `IoU(f(E(I), q), y) >= t_high` for every edit `E` whose support does not intersect `R` (subsumes the control clause of `N`; reported separately because it is the cheapest test).
- Text-side controls `T(I, q)`: `f(I, empty) != y` (a null expression must change the output); `f(I, swap_attr(q))` must move or return `none`; `f(I, shuffle(q))` is reported, not scored, because Cirik 2018 predicts most natural expressions survive shuffling.
- Necessity-plus `N+(R; I, q)`: `N` holds and the post-removal output is itself valid: either `none` when no instance satisfying `q` remains, or a new region `R'` with `S(R'; rem_R(I), q)`, where for that `S` call the judge sees `crop(R' ∪ supp(q))` with the inpainted hole `R` masked from its view, and the judge's flip rate on fallbacks adjacent to `R` is reported. `N` measures reliance; `N+` measures reliance with a correct fallback.
- Removal region. `R` for `rem_R` is the SAM 3 mask refined from the predicted box when box-to-mask IoU is at least 0.5; otherwise the box region itself is inpainted (rectangular hole) and the item is flagged `box-inpaint`. If the box contains several SAM 3 instances, the union is removed. The box-to-mask IoU distribution and the artifact gate result for each variant are reported.
- Duplicates and claim classes (Skeptic 92-M1). Let `M(q)` be the set of instances in `I` that satisfy the description in `q` (SAM 3 concept prompt on the head noun, each instance crop filtered by `J`). If `|M(q)| = 1`, necessity is removal of the cited region. If `|M(q)| > 1` and the cited region is in `M(q)`, the item is REDUNDANT (sufficient, correct, not individually necessary; reported, never penalized) and necessity is tested on the set: entailment or the box must die after all of `M(q)` is removed. For count claims, necessity holds if removing any one member of `M(q)` changes the judge's count. For relation claims the cited region is the pair and `M(q)` is the set of instance pairs satisfying the relation. Every necessity edit is paired with an artifact-matched control edit elsewhere; an item whose outcome flips under the control edit is UNRESOLVED and excluded from rewards and headline rates.
- Interventional consistency `IC(I, q) = mean{N, S, V, T}` over the operators applicable to `q` (`N+` reported alongside). Per-claim attribution class (IDEA-92): ATTRIBUTED = `S and N` (with or without the REDUNDANT flag); UNSUPPORTED = `not S` while `J` entails the claim from the full image (post-rationalized region); HALLUCINATED = `J` does not entail the claim from the full image; UNRESOLVED as above.
- Reward (IDEA-92): attributed-claim precision x coverage plus a utility term; REDUNDANT claims count as ATTRIBUTED in the numerator, the flag is reported, not scored.
- Natural-pair control. Real image pairs `(I_1, I_2)` with the same `q`, referent present in `I_1` and absent or different in `I_2` (consecutive video frames, multi-instance sets, VisMin-style verified pairs). `N` measured on natural pairs versus edited pairs; the discrepancy is the artifact effect and is reported in every table.

For description claims, `N` uses the judge rather than the grounder: `J(rem_{R}(I), c) < J(I, c) - delta_N` with `R` the cited region or, under the duplicates rule, all of `M(c)`, and `J(ctrl_{R'}(I), c) >= J(I, c) - epsilon` (Researcher3 confirms this translation in the log). Global claims (scene-level, no region) form a declared class verified against the full image, counted in coverage, excluded from attribution. The deletion and insertion operators are the ancestors used by IDEA-91: RISE `[VERIFIED by Verifier, IDEA-91 report: 1806.07421]` and critical-object masking in CSS `[VERIFIED by Verifier: 2003.06576]`. Ordering constraint shared with IDEA-91 (its Section 11): K1's artifact gate must pass on the shared editor before arm A's necessity term is trusted, and the edit bank is built once for both ideas where the pools coincide.

### 3.2 Output format and claim decomposition
Interleaved description with typed claim tags: `<claim id type=entity|attribute|count|relation|global region=[...]>text</claim>`; entities and attributes cite one region, relations two, counts a point set. For scoring, claims are decomposed by a text LLM into atomic facts, the FaithScore design `[VERIFIED by Verifier: 2311.01477]`, which Geigle also used as a hallucination measure; the model is trained to emit typed claims directly. Molmo2 emits points, so on the Molmo2 replicate a cited region is a point plus its SAM 3 mask; the region format differs between bases and is stated in every table (Researcher2).

### 3.3 Judge ensemble (Skeptic 92-M1, 92-M2; Researcher2 lineage rule)
Open judges only, never sharing lineage with the policy (IDEA-91 Section 3.1 rule): for the Qwen3-VL-4B policy, Gemma4-E4B as the reward-time judge and Gemma4-12B as the attribution-metric judge, with the hallucination outcome judge from a third lineage (InternVL3.5-14B), so no outcome detector shares lineage with the reward judge (92-E5); for the Molmo2-4B replicate, Qwen3.5-4B (reward), Qwen3.5-9B (metric) and Gemma4-12B (outcome), which swaps the roles so the two replicates check each other. The outcome judge's agreement with the human audit is reported. Molmo2 pointing for count claims. A blind-image contrast probe (BICR-style `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-141: 2605.10893]`) as an image-dependence gate on the judge. Resolution-matched crops (upsampled to the judge's native resolution) so sufficiency is not confounded by crop size; necessity confounded by context redundancy is handled by the control clause, the duplicates rule and by reporting `N` on natural pairs versus edits. Necessity edits use mask-conditioned inpainting from the claim's SAM 3 mask via IDEA-91's edit bank, with its forensic artifact gate; judge flip rates on control edits are a required table (92-M2). Necessity is computed on a claim subsample per rollout and on all claims at evaluation.

### 3.4 Training (serves the causal experiment)
Seed SFT at 4B on auto-attributed DenseWorld-1M captions (50k captions decomposed by a 9B text LLM, about 5 GPU-h; licence UNVERIFIABLE from the abstract page `[Verifier V-143: 2506.24102]`). The seed data's own four-class distribution and attributed precision are reported before training (92-M4), so RL gains are separable from seed quality. Then GRPO with
```
r = a * (#attributed / #scored)                 # attributed includes the redundant flag
  - b * (#hallucinated / #scored)
  - c * (#unsupported / #scored)
  - c' * (#unattributed non-global / #scored)   # a claim that cites no region when it could
  + d * coverage(reference claims) + e * utility(CapRL-style QA from the text) - f * [format error]
```
where #scored excludes UNRESOLVED claims; at most 12 claims are scored per rollout, chosen as a uniform random subsample of the rollout's claims, never the first 12 (92-M6); the `global` label is gated (92-M6): a claim tagged global whose head noun returns a SAM 3 instance in the image is re-typed as an entity claim and scored as unattributed non-global under c', the global fraction per rollout is reported per arm, and a pre-registered flag fires if any arm's global fraction exceeds twice the base model's; utility questions are precomputed per image; 300 GRPO steps; rollouts 600 to 1,000 tokens; judge on a dedicated GPU, batched; sufficiency precomputed per image over SAM 3 instances and online for off-bank regions (Researcher2's trims).

### 3.5 The causal experiment (Researcher3's P12; the headline; arm B per Skeptic 92-M3; arm labels follow Researcher3's log entry)
Same base, same data pool, same optimizer, same number of GRPO steps and judge calls (both budgets reported):
- (A) attribution reward above; three seeds.
- (B) region-free claim-verification GRPO: the same judge verifies the same atomic claims against the full image, no regions cited, ClaimDiff-RL / CuRe style `[VERIFIED by Verifier: 2605.20278, 2607.05150]`; three seeds. A minus B isolates region attribution with optimizer and judge fixed. Its reward, written out (92-M5):
```
r_B = a * (#true / #scored) - b * (#hallucinated / #scored)
    + d * coverage(reference claims) + e * utility(CapRL-style QA from the text) - f * [format error]
```
  with the same a, b, d, e, f as arm A, the same 12-claim uniform subsample, the same 300 steps and rollout-length limits, and no c or c' terms (there are no regions to be unattributed to); #true is the judge's full-image entailment. Scored-claim count and rollout length are reported per arm so A and B differ in regions only.
- (B') RLHF-V-style segment-level correction preference training `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-071: 2312.00849]` as a reference arm; it changes the optimizer as well and cannot carry the causal claim; compared with B to show what preference optimization adds independently of regions; one seed.
- (C) attribution reward without the necessity term (sufficiency only); one seed.
- (C') the Geigle arm, if budget allows: grounding-data SFT with noun-phrase boxes (GLaMM-style) on the same descriptions, no reward.
- (D) SFT-only on the attributed seed data (the data control); one seed.
- (E) the base model with a "describe with boxes" prompt.
Outcomes (92-E5): the primary causal outcome is the 1,000-claim human audit on arms A, B and E (span-level hallucination rate and the four-class distribution), because DetailVerifyBench's span labels cover its own captions and cannot label new generations. DetailVerifyBench is used two ways: its labelled captions validate the outcome judge (Gate 0a) and, at evaluation, the outcome judge of a third lineage (3.3) scores every arm's descriptions on DetailVerifyBench images so that the benchmark number is comparable across arms; the outcome judge's agreement with the human audit is reported and a judge of the reward judge's lineage is never used as an outcome. Secondary: RePOPE / DASH-B `[VERIFIED per Skeptic: 2504.15707]`, IoU of cited regions against the human audit, and MMMU / MMBench / POPE before and after.
Pre-registered readings (Researcher3 P1 to P3; Skeptic 92-E3):
- D and C' within CI of E on hallucination replicates Geigle.
- A below B with non-overlapping CIs and C between them: localization causally reduces hallucination and necessity is the operative term.
- A below B and C equal to A: sufficiency (region-conditioned verification) suffices and necessity is decorative.
- A below B and C equal to B: necessity is the operative term and sufficiency alone is decorative (92-E3).
- A within CI of B on hallucination and no better on the unsupported fraction: Geigle's null at the objective level; the paper is the metric, the audit of current models and the interface, and the causal claim is withdrawn in the file.
- B reduces hallucination but leaves the unsupported fraction unchanged: text-side verification does not make citations causal, reported whether or not A beats B.

### 3.6 Downstream justification of the interface (Skeptic 92-I1)
User study: 30 raters find hallucinations in 100 descriptions (about 200 words each) with and without clickable claim regions; outcomes are time to first detected error, error-detection rate, and false-alarm rate; click-to-region viewer and consent flow about two person-weeks (Researcher2). The pre-registered claim is a reduction in verification time of at least 30% at equal or better detection; the fraction of unsupported (post-rationalized) citations in current models is the headline motivating number if it is large.

## 4. Why novel and important

Delta: a causal, four-outcome attribution metric with a text-only prior baseline, a duplicates rule, control edits and a natural-pair control (no existing grounded-caption metric distinguishes attributed from redundant from post-rationalized citations; ViGoR's fine-grained rewards and FaithScore's atomic facts score claim truth, not region dependence), the first controlled experiment isolating whether region attribution causes lower hallucination in open generation with the judge and optimizer held fixed (answering Geigle 2024's null at the objective level rather than the data level), and a typed-claim interface with a measured downstream benefit.

Novelty test (three closest papers):
- KAWHI (2603.27375): region localization by hierarchical geometric aggregation, vision-critical attention heads and paragraph-level credit reallocation inside GRPO/GSPO, for reasoning benchmarks `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-154]`. This idea does not claim the reward; it defines attribution causally (necessity with control and duplicates rule, prior-adjusted sufficiency), classifies claims four ways, targets description rather than reasoning, and runs the equal-data causal comparison KAWHI does not.
- COPO (2508.04182): token-level causal sufficiency and necessity as a completeness reward inside GRPO `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-155]`. This idea applies necessity and sufficiency to image regions cited per claim with edit-based interventions and control edits, and measures the class distribution on existing models.
- ClaimDiff-RL (2605.20278): reference-conditioned atomic visual-claim differences verified against the image as the reward unit, with a 160-image human diagnostic set; no region attribution `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-156]`. This idea attributes each claim to a region and tests the region, and uses ClaimDiff-style region-free verification as the control arm to isolate localization's causal role.

## 5. Closest related work

- ViGoR (2402.06118): fine-grained reward modelling from human and automated evaluations to improve the visual grounding of LVLM descriptions, 15,440 evaluated image-text pairs `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`. Delta: ViGoR scores description quality finely but does not attach a region to each claim or test the region's necessity; it is the reward-modelling baseline a reviewer will name and is reported as such.
- GROUNDHOG (2402.16846, CVPR 2024): grounded description with entity masks from holistic segmentation (M3G2), claims reduced object hallucination `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`. Delta: entity masks by SFT, overlap-scored; no claim-level verification, no necessity, no controlled comparison against region-free training.
- FaithScore (2311.01477): reference-free atomic-fact extraction and verification against the image `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`. Delta: FaithScore is the decomposition-plus-judge evaluation this idea reuses for claim truth; it has no region and cannot separate attributed from post-rationalized citations.
- Woodpecker (2310.16045): training-free post-hoc correction with key-concept extraction, question formulation, detector validation and claim correction `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`. Delta: post-hoc correction with a verifier ensemble; this idea trains with the verifier and tests necessity.
- CuRe (2607.05150): category-aware atomic claims through a structured rubric for video-caption RL; region scope not stated `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-146]`. Structured rewards with an instance-aware scene-graph reward and a video-grounded VQA reward for self-verification (2604.01460) `[VERIFIED by Verifier: listing]`. Rubric rewards for long-form hallucination RL (2608.12337) `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-196]`. All claim-level verified rewards without per-claim region attribution; the arm-B family.
- Geigle et al. 2024 (2406.14492): the null result this idea tests at the objective level, and the inference-time grounded-caption data point.
- GAVEL (2606.26923): verification, explanation and localization of caption errors; the SFT-on-GAVEL-train baseline is mandatory and necessary but not sufficient.
- GLaMM (2311.03356), DenseWorld-1M (2506.24102), Describe Anything (2504.16072) `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-142 to V-144]`: grounded and localized captioning; data and baselines.
- Ground What You See (2601.06224): RL can increase hallucination (three causes); fix with planning and captioning stages, a caption-quality reward, diversity prioritization and an InfoNCE regularizer `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-139]`; whether outputs carry per-claim regions is not described in the abstract. Baseline.
- CapRL (2509.22647): reward = vision-free LLM answering MCQs from the caption; no regions `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-145]`; utility term.
- SCCM / faithfulness of visual thinking (2510.23482), VISE (2606.27373; geometric-invariance GIoU reward and a blur-based semantic-invariance reward on the model's own predicted box, evaluated on captioning and VQA `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-153]`): sufficiency and invariance notions; contrasts.
- RISE (1806.07421), CSS (2003.06576): deletion and insertion ancestors of the necessity operator, shared with IDEA-91.
- FActScore (2305.14251): the text-side atomic-fact analogue `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-147]`.
- DetailVerifyBench (2604.05623), RePOPE (2504.15707): evaluation. RLHF-V (2312.00849): arm B'. BICR (2605.10893): judge image-dependence gate. PND (2604.24396): decoding-time baseline. Over-alignment (2605.08245) has both a training-free projection and a bias-aware fine-tuning variant `[VERIFIED by Verifier V-196]`; the training-free variant is a decoding-time baseline and the fine-tuning variant an extra training reference.

## 6. Experimental validation

Benchmarks (Verifier A1) and contamination (Verifier A2): DetailVerifyBench (1,000 images, 5 domains; span-level), GAVEL (human splits), RePOPE / DASH-B for object hallucination (POPE itself is saturated and label-noisy, A4 and A1), a new attributed-precision set of 1,000 post-2024 images (SA-1B / OpenImages) with a 1,000-claim human audit (three annotators, agreement reported), natural-pair controls from consecutive video frames and multi-instance sets, and GroundingME plus Ref-L4 Acc@0.5/0.75/0.9 to show region quality did not regress. CHAIR, AMBER and RefCOCO in an appendix only. Bases: Qwen3-VL-4B (COCO and RefCOCO in its mix, so COCO-derived hallucination sets are image-shared) and Molmo2-4B (no RefCOCO annotations in its documented mixture; COCO images present via VQA-style sets `[PARTIALLY, Verifier V-112]`) for clean-image claims. A4 dependence: (b) REFUTED as a general claim is the premise being tested; (a) motivates the appendix placement of RefCOCO.

Baselines: arm E; arm D (SFT on auto-attributed DenseWorld-1M) and C'; SFT on GAVEL train; GROUNDHOG- and GLaMM-style entity-mask grounding; CapRL utility-only RL; arms B and B'; decoding-time fixes; free baselines: sampling plus NMS voting on cited regions, max_pixels sweep, token-likelihood confidence per claim.

Metrics: attributed (with redundant share) / unsupported / hallucinated / unresolved fractions by claim type; text-only prior `J_text` per claim; judge flip rate on control edits and the natural-pair versus edit discrepancy; claim specificity (92-E2): type distribution, share of entity-only claims, mean information per claim (judge's `J_text` as the inverse-specificity proxy), claims per 100 words; coverage; utility; DetailVerifyBench span rate; RePOPE/DASH-B; human-audited attributed precision; user-study outcomes; general-capability regression (MMMU, MMBench, POPE).

Ablations: necessity term (arm C); prior-adjusted vs raw sufficiency; duplicates rule on versus off (the off variant reproduces the mislabelling of 92-M1 and is reported to show its size); typed vs untyped claims; judge choice and size; sufficiency precomputed vs online; global-claim class removed.

Expected effect sizes `[SPECULATION]`: on current models, 30 to 50% of cited regions unsupported or redundant by the four-class metric; arm A reducing DetailVerifyBench span rate by a third relative to D and by at least 10% relative to B; user verification time down 30%.

Kill experiment (numeric stop rules; Skeptic 92-E1 and the lead), before any training, about 5 GPU-h plus about 150 USD:
- Gate 0a (claim truth): attribute existing Qwen3-VL-8B descriptions post hoc (LLM claim decomposition plus SAM 3 regions), run the crop judge with prior adjustment, and compare with DetailVerifyBench human span labels. Stop if the judge's AUROC for attribute and relation claims is below 0.75, or below 0.80 for entity claims; if only attributes and relations fail, restrict the reward and metric to entity and count claims.
- Gate 0b (region attribution, 92-E1): a 300-claim human-labelled attribution set, stratified by claim type (entity, attribute, count, relation), three annotators; the claim-level twin of IDEA-91's K3 protocol, run together with it if the audits coincide (Researcher3). Humans label what they can judge reliably: whether the crop alone shows the claim (sufficiency), how many other instances in the image satisfy the claim (the size of `M(c)`), and whether the claim is true of the full image; the necessity label is then derived from the removal edit under the duplicates rule and checked against the human duplicate count. Gate: Cohen's kappa between the judge-derived class and the human-derived class of at least 0.6 for entity and count claims and at least 0.5 for attribute and relation claims, per type; a type that fails is excluded from the reward and reported only. What Gate 0b validates (92-E4): the judge's sufficiency call `S` and the duplicate count `|M(c)|`, not necessity itself, since humans cannot label necessity directly; `N` is checked on the same 300 claims by reporting the discrepancy between natural-pair and edited-pair `N` (the artifact effect) per claim type, and a type whose discrepancy exceeds 10 points is flagged before training.
- Gate 0c (motivation): if the four-class metric on current models finds fewer than 15% unsupported-or-redundant citations, the interface motivation is weak and the paper reduces to the causal experiment alone.

## 10. Paper pitch (Phase 3 input; conditional on Gates 0a to 0c and IDEA-91's K1, which run before the seed SFT)

**Working title.** Does Localization Cause Faithfulness? Attributed Description with Necessary and Sufficient Evidence.

**Abstract-style claim.** Grounding is assumed to reduce hallucination, but the one controlled test finds that grounding objectives at training time have little to no effect on caption hallucination. We ask a sharper question: when a model must cite a region for every claim and is rewarded only if that region is sufficient (a frozen judge entails the claim from the crop, above its text-only prior) and necessary (removing the region, or all duplicate instances, breaks entailment while a matched control edit does not), does hallucination fall relative to the same judge verifying the same claims without regions? We introduce a four-outcome attribution metric (attributed with a redundant flag, unsupported, hallucinated, unresolved) that shows what fraction of current models' cited regions are post-rationalized, run the controlled experiment at equal data, steps and judge calls, and measure the interface's value in a human verification study.

**Three contributions a reviewer would list.**
1. A causal attribution metric for grounded descriptions with a text-only prior baseline, a duplicates rule, control edits and a natural-pair control, and its audit of current models (the post-rationalized fraction).
2. The first controlled experiment isolating region attribution from claim verification, answering Geigle 2024's null at the objective level with pre-registered readings for every outcome.
3. A typed-claim interface whose downstream value (verification time, error-detection rate) is measured rather than asserted.

**Headline figure and table.** Figure 1: four-class distribution of cited regions for current models by claim type (entity, attribute, count, relation), with the text-only prior shown per class. Table 1: the causal experiment, arms A, B, B', C, C', D, E, with DetailVerifyBench span rate, RePOPE/DASH-B, human-audited attributed precision, the unsupported fraction and the general-capability regression, three seeds for A and B with CIs.

**The single result that makes the paper.** Arm A below arm B on DetailVerifyBench span rate by 10 to 20% relative with non-overlapping CIs, the unsupported fraction falling by at least 10 points under A and unchanged under B, and arm C between A and B `[SPECULATION: predicted ranges]`. Stop rules (Section 6): Gate 0a judge AUROC below 0.75 (attribute and relation) or 0.80 (entity), Gate 0b per-type kappa below 0.6 (entity, count) or 0.5 (attribute, relation), and Gate 0c fewer than 15% unsupported-or-redundant citations in current models; if A is within CI of B on hallucination and no better on the unsupported fraction, the causal claim is withdrawn and the paper is the metric, the audit and the interface.
