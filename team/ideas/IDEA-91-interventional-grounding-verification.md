## 0. What the paper is

Three contributions in a fixed order, because the Skeptic rates the first two WORTH DEVELOPING and the third CROWDED unless the first two carry it.

1. **An audit.** Current REC grounders and grounded-chain-of-thought reasoners are tested under interventions anchored on *their own predictions*: edit outside the predicted region (invariance), remove the predicted region (necessity), judge the crop alone (sufficiency), and perturb the expression (text-side controls). The audit answers a question the field has asked since 2018 and never answered for localization outputs: how many IoU-correct boxes are right for the wrong reason.
2. **A metric.** The interventional consistency score `IC` is label-free, so it can be computed on any image, including post-training-cutoff images that no model has seen. The paper shows `IC` agrees with human judgment on a validated subset and that `IC` measured on unlabelled post-cutoff images tracks accuracy on labelled hard benchmarks (Ref-L4 at IoU 0.75, GroundingME): primarily at the item level, where `IC` must beat the judge `S` alone and a two-seed disagreement predictor (R2: 91-E6, 91-E7), and secondarily across at least 70 nominal model units with a cluster bootstrap over training runs and base lineages (R1: 91-M4; R2: 91-E4). That is the property a contamination-resistant grounding benchmark needs and no existing benchmark has. If `IC` does not beat `S` alone, the paper says the metric is the judge and ships as the audit.
3. **A training signal (secondary).** The same operators become a GRPO reward on unlabeled, COCO-free images. The paper runs the direct arm the Skeptic demanded: realistic prediction-conditioned edits with control edits versus gray-mask counterfactuals (DeFacto-style), blur-ghosting with a visibility reward (VISE-style), attention-masked blind states (VIGIL-style) and a corrupted-image KL (PAPO-style) at equal compute. If any cheap counterfactual matches, the training contribution is dropped and the paper ships as contributions 1 and 2.

## 1. Research problem

**What is broken.** Grounding is evaluated by IoU against a reference box, which cannot distinguish a model that found the referent from one that found the only salient object, the largest instance of the head noun, or the location the training set favours. The evidence that this matters is old and consistent: an image-only model reaches 71.2% top-2 precision on RefCOCOg and a category-only model 84.2% `[VERIFIED: arXiv 1805.11818 abstract, Verifier V-068]`; a training-free "moment prior" beats trained models on Charades-STA `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2101.09028, V-074]`; attention supervision that appeared to teach VQA models to look at the right place worked as well with random cues `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2004.05704, V-069]`; and in 2025-2026 the same control, applied to reasoning traces and tool observations, found that visual evidence cited in multimodal chain-of-thought is largely not load-bearing (predictions nearly unchanged under visual intervention, changed under textual intervention `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2510.23482 abstract]`; tool-use gains concentrated in a "calibrated minority", with "calling without looking" as a named failure mode `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2608.06270 abstract]`). No prediction-anchored, image-level control with abstention semantics has been run on *localization outputs*: does the box move, or become "none", when the referent itself is gone? The nearest ancestors, which a reviewer will supply, are deletion / insertion metrics for saliency maps (RISE, arXiv 1806.07421), counterfactual training samples built by masking critical objects (CSS, arXiv 2003.06576), object transplanting as an invariance test for detectors (The Elephant in the Room, arXiv 1808.03305), and false-premise referring expressions with a "say" correction (FP-RefCOCO / SESAME, arXiv 2312.08366) `[VERIFIED by Verifier: verifications/IDEA-91-verifier.md Section B]`; none anchors an image edit on the model's own predicted region and scores the localization output.

**Why the benchmarks cannot answer it.** The canonical benchmarks are saturated (top open models at 92-96 on RefCOCO val/testA with a 1-2 point spread inside the label-noise band `[VERIFIED: Verifier anchors A1, A4(a)]`), noisy (label error 14 / 24 / 5% on RefCOCO / RefCOCO+ / RefCOCOg `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2406.16866, V-015]`), contaminated (Qwen3-VL trains on RefCOCO/+/g, COCO, Objects365, OpenImages and Grounding-DINO pseudo-labels `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2511.21631 Section 3.2.4, V-004; anchors A2]`), and single-threshold. The 2025-2026 hard benchmarks (GroundingME: best of 25 MLLMs at 45.1%; 20 of 25 at exactly 0% on rejection in non-thinking mode (rejection scored as a literal null-box output), some rejection in thinking mode, and a 2:1 positive/negative fine-tune of Qwen3-VL-8B reaching 27.9% `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2512.17495 body, V-111]`; OpenRef with none-target and multi-target items `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2605.25706 abstract]`; Ref-Adv 2026, on which Qwen2.5-VL-72B falls from 92.7 on RefCOCO to 54.1 `[VERIFIED: anchors A4(a), arXiv 2602.23898]`) are curated and finite, so they will be trained on within a year, like every debiased split before them (VQA-CP, Charades-CD, Ref-Adv 2020).

**Who cares.** Anyone who acts on a predicted box: GUI agents, robot pointing, grounded captioning, and every RL-with-IoU-reward paper, whose rewards inherit whatever prior the reference boxes carry. And anyone who needs a grounding benchmark that survives contamination.

## 2. Core insight

A predicted region is a falsifiable hypothesis, and image editing makes it cheap to falsify. Prior audits intervened on dataset items chosen by the benchmark author; VISE (2026) anchors a perturbation on the model's own predicted box but rewards a *visibility judgment* under a blur that is detectable by construction. Anchoring a *realistic* intervention on the model's own output, pairing it with an artifact-matched control edit, and requiring the *localization output itself* (box, `none`, or a set) to respond, turns any image into a test item that is specific to what the model actually did and cannot be passed by detecting the edit. Four relations follow: the output must not move when the image is edited outside the referent (**invariance**), must change, and change to a valid fallback, when the referent is removed (**necessity**, `N` and `N+`), must be confirmable from the region alone (**sufficiency**), and must respond to the expression (**text-side controls**). Invariance and necessity measure causal reliance; sufficiency measures correctness by a judge; together they approximate "correct and for the right reason" with no reference box. Lineage, stated so no reviewer has to: necessity and sufficiency are the deletion and insertion metrics of saliency-map evaluation (RISE, arXiv 1806.07421 `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`) transplanted from explanation maps to localization outputs, and invariance under transplanted objects was run on detectors in 2018 (arXiv 1808.03305 `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`); the new elements are the anchoring on the model's own output, the abstention semantics, the control edits, and the use of the relations as a validated metric and reward.

## 3. Proposed methodology

Notation is the shared block in `landscape/r3-history.md` Section 7: image `I`, expression `q`, output `y = f(I, q)` in {box or mask, `none`, set of boxes}; operators `rem_R`, `ctrl_R'`, `crop_R`, `move_R`, `ins_R''`; judges `J(I, q)` and `J_text(q)`; relations `N`, `N+`, `S`, `V`, `T`; score `IC`; the removal-region rule; the duplicates rule with REDUNDANT and UNRESOLVED.

### 3.1 Expression support, the removal region, and the four relations

For expressions that mention context objects ("the cup left of the laptop"), an edit that removes the laptop *should* move the box, so it is not an invariance test. Define the **expression support** `supp(q)` as the union of SAM 3 concept-prompt instances for every noun phrase in `q` (parser: an open LLM; instances: SAM 3 concept prompts `[VERIFIED per Researcher2: github.com/facebookresearch/sam3]`).

**Removal region (R1: 91-M2).** `R` is the SAM 3 mask refined from the predicted box when box-to-mask IoU is at least 0.5; otherwise the box region itself is inpainted and the item is flagged `box-inpaint`; if the box contains several SAM 3 instances, their union is removed. The box-to-mask IoU distribution is reported per model, and K1's artifact gate is run separately on mask holes and rectangular holes, since rectangular holes are more detectable.

- **Invariance `V`**: edits with support disjoint from `supp(q)` and from `R`: control removals of matched size, distractor insertions elsewhere, background edits. Pass if `IoU(f(E(I), q), y) >= t_high` (default 0.7). This is the least-claimed relation for REC and MLLM outputs (object transplanting was run on detectors in 2018, arXiv 1808.03305; VISE's geometric invariance transforms the whole image, Section 4) and the cheapest to run on existing models, so the audit leads with it.
- **Necessity `N` and `N+` (R1: 91-M3)**: `rem_R(I)` as above. `N` passes if `f(rem_R(I), q) = none`, or the new output has `IoU < t_low` (0.3) with `R`, **and** the model passes `V` on the matched control edit. `N+` additionally requires the post-removal output to be valid: `none` when no instance satisfying `q` remains, or a new region `R'` with `S(R'; rem_R(I), q)`; for that `S` call the judge sees `crop(R' ∪ supp(q))` with the inpainted hole `R` masked from its view, and the judge's flip rate on fallbacks adjacent to `R` is reported (R2: 91-M8). `N` measures reliance; `N+` is the relation used for the headline right-box-wrong-reason rate, because a random move and a correct fallback to the next instance are different behaviours.
- **Sufficiency `S`**: `J(crop(R ∪ supp(q)), q) >= theta` with the crop upsampled to the judge's native resolution. The judge is a frozen open VLM served on its own GPU (Qwen3.5-9B or Gemma4-12B class `[per Researcher2 8.2]`), never the policy or its lineage.
- **Text-side `T`**: `f(I, empty) != y` (null expression must change the output); `f(I, swap_attr(q))` and `f(I, swap_head(q))` must move or return `none`; `f(I, shuffle(q))` is reported, not scored, because natural expressions mostly survive shuffling `[VERIFIED: V-068]`.

`IC(I, q)` is the mean of the applicable relations; `N+` is reported alongside. Duplicates follow the shared rule: if several instances satisfy `q`, the item is REDUNDANT and necessity is tested on the set; items whose outcome flips under the control edit are UNRESOLVED and excluded from rewards and headline rates. Every relation is computed with the **always-edited design** (Researcher2 8.10): every image in a pool, including factual positives, carries a control edit of matched mask size on a non-referent region by the same editor, so "was this image edited" is uninformative by construction.

**Dependence on SAM 3 (R1: 91-A1).** `supp(q)`, the edit bank and distractor insertion all use SAM 3 instances. On the human-validated subset the paper reports SAM 3 recall of the referent and of `supp(q)` entities, and recomputes `IC` with `supp(q)` from a second detector (Grounding DINO or OWLv2) to give a sensitivity band. SAM 3's custom licence governs the derived edit bank (Section 7).

### 3.3 Part B: the metric and its validation (contribution 2)

- **Human validation (K3).** 1,000 items stratified across sources; three annotators label "box correct", "box correct but expression ambiguous", "box wrong"; report agreement between `IC` components and human labels (targets: Cohen's kappa >= 0.6 between `S` and "box correct"; AUROC >= 0.85 of `IC` for predicting human "box correct" among IoU@0.5-correct items). `S` carries correctness, `N+` and `V` carry reliance, and their conjunction is validated against people before any benchmark claim. Human evidence data inherited from IDEA-31 (AiR, VQA-MHUG, DeFacto-1.5K, COCO-Search18 fixations) and any click-based evidence marks collected for this validation are described as *human evidence marks*, not gaze, and are used only to validate the score, never as a training signal (Skeptic note in `critiques/IDEA-31-skeptic.md`, accepted).
- **Predictive validity, two designs (R1: 91-M4).** (i) *Item level (primary):* per-item `IC` computed on the labelled hard benchmarks predicts per-item correctness at Ref-L4 IoU 0.75 and on GroundingME; AUROC with item-level bootstrap CIs; target >= 0.80. **Decomposition (R2: 91-E6):** AUROC is reported for `S` alone, for `N+` and `V` alone, and for `IC`, on the same items, together with calibration (ECE) and AUROC on the subset where `S` is wrong (the right-box-wrong-reason cases); the metric contribution is claimed only if `IC` beats `S` alone by at least 0.03 AUROC overall or by at least 0.05 on the `S`-wrong subset, otherwise the metric is the judge and contribution 1 carries the paper. Item-level baselines: `S` alone; verification-based zero-shot REC (arXiv 2509.09958 `[VERIFIED per Skeptic: listing]`); OpenRef's consistency checker (arXiv 2605.25706). (ii) *Unit level (secondary; R2: 91-E4):* units are every audited base model, every size within a family (Qwen3-VL 2B/4B/8B/32B; InternVL3.5 sizes; Molmo2 4B/8B), and intermediate GRPO checkpoints from every training arm (5 checkpoints x 4 arms x 3 seeds = 60), giving n >= 70 *nominal* units. Checkpoints along one run are correlated and arm (c) is trained to raise `IC`, so the Spearman is computed with a cluster bootstrap (clusters = training run, i.e. arm x seed, and base lineage), the effective sample size is reported, and three versions are given: all units; without arm (c) checkpoints; and base models and sizes only (about 10 to 12 units, labelled underpowered). Spearman correlation, with cluster-bootstrap CI, between `IC` on GroundLive (unlabelled, post-cutoff) and Ref-L4 Acc@0.75 (its Objects365-test subset for Qwen3-VL bases, since the COCO part is contaminated) or GroundingME accuracy (labelled, pre-cutoff); target: CI lower bound >= 0.5. **Baseline predictor (Verifier):** label-free accuracy estimation from disagreement has a statistical lineage (Jiang, Nagarajan, Baek, Kolter, arXiv 2106.13799 `[VERIFIED by Verifier]`), so both designs compare `IC` against a sample-disagreement predictor (disagreement between two seeds or two sampling temperatures of the same model on the same unlabelled items); `IC` must beat it, otherwise the edits add nothing over self-consistency. The claim is "`IC` on unlabelled post-cutoff images tracks labelled hard-benchmark accuracy", not "`IC` beats a saturated number"; RefCOCO Acc@0.5 is reported for context only.
- **GroundLive, circularity removed (R1: 91-M5).** Images: Creative-Commons uploads with an upload date after the latest training cutoff among all audited models (one date, so "post-cutoff" holds for every model), de-duplicated by perceptual hash against COCO, Objects365, OpenImages, SA-1B and the LAION-derived sets we can access, and excluding COCO-like re-uploads flagged by reverse-image search on a sample. Expressions: written and verified on the factual image by a generator of a *different lineage* from the `S` judge (Gemma4-class writes, Qwen3.5-class judges, and the reverse split is run as a check), so `S` is not scored by the model that selected the items. Difficulty target: the pool is stratified to match GroundingME's referent-size and SAM 3 instance-count distribution rather than the captioner's salience bias, and the `S` pass rate per stratum is reported against the 200-item human anchor per refresh. The paper releases the generator, not a fixed set.

### 3.4 Natural pairs (no editing artifacts at all)

- **COCO-Search18** target-present / target-absent pairs for 18 categories: 3,101 target-present and 3,101 target-absent real images, about 300K fixations, 10 subjects per category; target-present fixations are released, target-absent fixations are not yet public, so the *images* of both halves are usable as natural pairs now and scanpath comparisons use target-present items only; MIT-style licence with non-commercial and no-redistribution terms `[VERIFIED: V-133]`. Category-level referring where the absent case is a *real* photo (evaluation only; COCO images, so contaminated for Qwen3-VL, reported for Molmo2 as the cleaner lineage).
- **Video exits.** Consecutive frames from SA-V / Ego4D-class video where a tracked object leaves the frame; same expression, referent present then absent.
- **Multi-instance photos.** Real images with two instances of the head noun where only one satisfies the attribute; the "other instance" is the natural distractor and the natural test of `N+`.
- **VisMin** human-verified edited pairs `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2407.16772]` as an edited-but-verified middle tier.
`N` and `N+` on natural pairs versus edited pairs are reported side by side in every table; their discrepancy is the artifact effect.

### 3.5 Diagnostic arm: priors under outcome rewards (former NF-5)

"Blind" is a **box-prior model**, not a text-only model: (i) centre / median-size prior; (ii) head-noun prior: SAM 3 with the head noun only, return the largest or most central instance; (iii) training-set location histogram (the Charades-CD construction, arXiv 2101.09028). Run current RL grounders (Visual-RFT / VLM-R1 / Time-R1 class) on iid versus OOD: Charades-CD OOD with TimeLens re-annotations `[per Researcher2; TimeLens arXiv 2512.14698]`, both Ref-Adv sets, and null-expression inputs. **Decision rule:** if the iid-to-OOD gap exceeds 10 points (Acc@0.5 or R@1) on any split, or the null-expression hit rate exceeds the box-prior model's by less than 5 points, a prior-relative advantage becomes a section of the training half; otherwise it is one diagnostic table. PAPO already implements "reward only where the modality matters" as an implicit perception KL term `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2507.06448 abstract; the corrupted-image construction is body-level, PARTIALLY per Verifier]`, so the method is not claimed as new; the localization-specific diagnostic is.

### 3.6 Part C: training (contribution 3, secondary)

- **Policy and base.** Qwen3-VL-4B (Apache-2.0, EasyR1-native, 0-1000 coordinates `[VERIFIED: V-003; per Researcher2 8.9]`), with Molmo2-4B as the RefCOCO-free replicate.
- **Data.** 20k-image COCO-free pool (SA-1B and OpenImages; Objects365 *train* may be added, since Ref-L4 uses Objects365 *test* images, which is domain overlap but not image overlap `[VERIFIED: V-134]`; results are reported with and without it). Expressions: captioner + LLM write one expression per SAM 3 instance, verified on the factual image by the different-lineage verifier of Section 3.3; no human boxes used for the reward.
- **Edit bank (Researcher2 8.10).** Per image, SAM 3 instances (about 8); one LaMa-class removal each; two attribute swaps with a 4B instruction editor; one distractor insertion (copy-paste of a same-category instance, POBF-style `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2412.00684, V-076]`); judge verification of every edit by the edit verifier (a different model from the reward judge); matched control edits for all. Rewards are lookups keyed by the bank instance whose mask best overlaps the predicted box; the fast inpainter is the fallback for off-bank boxes.
- **Reward.** `r = IC(I, q) - lambda * [always-abstain penalty]`, with the positive-rate constraint that `P(y != none)` on factual images stays at or above the base rate; UNRESOLVED items contribute zero; plus IoU where labels exist for the labelled-data replicate.
- **Judge inside the reward (R1: 91-M6).** `S` uses judge A (Qwen3.5-9B) during training; evaluation reports `S` under judge A, under a held-out judge B (Gemma4-12B), and against human labels; a policy whose `S` gain under A exceeds its gain under B by more than 5 points, or whose human-validated `S` does not move, is reported as judge-gamed `[VERIFIED per Skeptic: judge gaming, arXiv 2607.09492]`.
- **The direct arm (R1: 91-N1; Skeptic 6.3 D-R1b objection 1).** Same pipeline, same compute, five counterfactual generators: (a) gray mask over `R` (DeFacto-style); (b) blur-ghosting of `R` with VISE's visibility reward and its geometric-invariance reward (VISE-style, Section 4); (b') attention-masked blind state with a blind-confidence penalty (VIGIL-style); (c) realistic prediction-conditioned edit with control edits (ours); (d) PAPO's corrupted-image KL. Pre-registered prediction: (a), (b) and (b') can be satisfied by detecting the degraded region (none of them has a control edit, so "degraded implies absent" is a valid shortcut under their rewards) and therefore match (c) on edited evaluation sets but not on natural-pair `N+`, GroundingME rejection, or invariance; if for any of (a), (b), (b') the 95% CI of the (c) minus arm difference on natural-pair `N+` or GroundingME rejection includes 2 points, contribution 3 is dropped (K5).

### 3.6a Researcher1: edit operators, online edit server, control-edit protocol, Syn-GRPO precedent, kill experiment

(Merged from `ideas/IDEA-91-R1-section-edit-server.md`, Researcher1's text, cross-referenced; that file remains the source of record. Researcher1's ACCEPT entries for 91-M2 and 91-E1 on this section are in the Discussion log, applied in S2 and S7 of their file.)

**S1. Edit operators and what each one licenses as a verifiable label.** Given an image `I`, an expression `e`, and a region proposal `B` (the policy's own box, or a SAM 3 instance during precompute), the edit server produces variants with labels known by construction:

| Operator | Edit | Required output on the edited image | Reward test |
|---|---|---|---|
| REMOVE | inpaint the referent's SAM 3 mask | `none` | rejection; and on the original, the box must overlap the edited mask (else the model grounded something else) |
| SWAP | change the attribute named in `e` (colour, material, state) on the referent while keeping its shape | `none` if no other instance now matches; else the new match | tests that `e`'s discriminating attribute is read from pixels |
| INSERT | paste a look-alike instance elsewhere (or duplicate the referent) | two boxes ("multiple") | tests exhaustiveness and multi-target output |
| RELOCATE | cut the referent and inpaint at a new position | box at the new position | tests location prior vs pixels (the necessity operator of Section 3.1) |
| CONTROL | same-size inpaint or paste on a non-referent region | unchanged output | invariance `V`; and the artefact gate (S3) |

Text-side operators (Section 3.1 `T`) are applied to the same image and share the reward table.

**S2. Online edit server design.** Precompute, not per rollout (Researcher2 blocker 1): for each image in the pool (SA-1B, OpenImages, Objects365; no COCO), run SAM 3 concept prompts over a noun list to get 5 to 15 instances with masks and categories; for each instance precompute REMOVE, one SWAP (attribute chosen by a captioner), one RELOCATE and one CONTROL, and for each category with at least one instance one INSERT; store edits and masks in a bank keyed by image and instance. Reward-time selection: for a rollout box `V`, pick the bank instance whose mask best matches `V` (IoU above 0.5); its variants become the counterfactual set; if no instance matches, fall back to a fast mask-conditioned inpainter (LaMa-class, about 50 ms per megapixel image) on `V` itself, logged as "off-bank" so its share is reported. Editors: removal and relocation with LaMa-class inpainting; attribute swap and insertion with Qwen-Image-Edit / Qwen-Image-Lightning (Apache-2.0, 4 to 8 steps) or FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache-2.0); budget 0.5 to 2 s per edit, about 30 to 60 GPU-h per 100k edits `[per Researcher2 8.2]`. Edit verification: a frozen open VLM checks each edit (referent absent after REMOVE; attribute changed after SWAP; new instance present after INSERT; nothing referent-related changed after CONTROL); failed edits are discarded and the failure rate per operator is reported; the verifier is a different model from the reward judge. Asynchrony: the bank is built ahead of training; a small asynchronous worker (Syn-GRPO's data-server pattern, S4) tops up off-bank edits for boxes the policy proposes frequently, so bank coverage rises over training. Licences: SAM 3 is under a custom licence, not Apache/MIT; check redistribution terms before releasing the edit bank; the editors listed are Apache-2.0 `[LIKELY per Researcher2]`.

**S3. Control-edit protocol (artefact shortcuts).** The failure to pre-empt: the policy learns "image was edited, so say none". (1) Every REMOVE/SWAP/RELOCATE variant is paired with a CONTROL variant of matched size and editor on a non-referent region of the same image; the required output on CONTROL is the original box; rewards are computed on the pair, so an "edited implies none" policy scores zero on half the pair. (2) Detectability gate before any RL: train a ResNet-18 to classify referent-edited vs control-edited images on 2k held-out pairs; if AUROC exceeds about 0.6, the reward is exploitable; fix the editor or the masking recipe before proceeding (about 2 GPU-h); re-run the gate on the policy's off-bank edits at the end of training. (3) Positive controls with artefacts: a fraction of ORIGINAL images are passed through an identity edit (encode-decode through the editor) so that editor fingerprints appear on positives too. (4) Report per-operator abstention rates on CONTROL images (should match the original) and the classifier AUROC in the paper's main table. (Section 6 K1 adds the ViT-S probe, the forensic-detector adversary, and the behavioural gate.)

**S4. Syn-GRPO precedent and what differs.** Syn-GRPO `[VERIFIED per Researcher1: arXiv 2511.19343]` places an image generation model inside the GRPO loop as a decoupled, asynchronous data server that synthesizes new training images from descriptions, with a diversity reward. We borrow the decoupled asynchronous server and the "generator inside the loop" engineering. We differ in purpose and in supervision: Syn-GRPO synthesizes whole images for diversity and still needs task labels for correctness; IDEA-91 synthesizes targeted counterfactuals of the policy's own proposals whose masks are known by construction, so the edit itself is the label and no human box is needed. DeFacto `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2509.20912]` and the corrupted-image preference family (mDPO, POVID, OViP 2505.15963, P2-DPO 2606.03376, ViPSy 2606.28401 `[VERIFIED per Researcher1: 2606.28401 abstract only]`) prepare masked or varied images offline for answer- or caption-level preferences; none conditions the edit on the model's predicted region, none targets localization outputs, none uses control edits against artefact shortcuts. (VISE, Section 4, conditions a blur on the predicted box but rewards a visibility judgment and has no control edits.)

**S5. Kill experiment K2 (numeric stop rules; Researcher1).** Build 2k edit pairs (REMOVE plus CONTROL) on GroundingME and OpenRef images with SAM 3 masks and the editors above. Stop rules, evaluated before any training: (1) detectability: ResNet-18 AUROC referent-edit vs control-edit above 0.6 after one editor iteration, stop (the reward is exploitable); (2) discriminativeness: if Qwen3-VL-8B and Molmo2-8B already output `none` on more than 60% of REMOVE images under the native grounding prompt, the negatives are too easy and the rejection half has no headroom; if they hallucinate a box on more than 80% of REMOVE images but also abstain on more than 30% of CONTROL images, the edits are confusing rather than informative, stop and fix editors; (3) consistency headroom: on ORIGINAL images where the model's box is correct, the fraction of REMOVE images on which it re-predicts the same box must exceed 20% for the faithfulness half to have headroom; below that, keep only the rejection half. Cost about 10 GPU-h. (R1: 91-E1, ACCEPTED by Researcher1 in the Discussion log: K2 records judge-verified removal success per operator and a 200-item human check of removal quality; the "emits a box on removal" rate is reported only on human-verified clean removals, and editor-remnant responses are a separate row.)

## 4. Why novel and important

The delta is **not** "the anchor" (R1: 91-N1; VISE anchors a perturbation on the predicted box). The delta is: (i) the object under test is the *localization output* with box / `none` / set semantics, not a visibility judgment or an answer; (ii) realistic edits paired with artifact-matched **control edits**, so the relation cannot be passed by detecting a degraded region; (iii) **contextual invariance** (edit outside the referent, image otherwise unchanged) rather than geometric invariance under whole-image transforms; (iv) `N+`, sufficiency and text-side controls; (v) validation against human labels and **natural pairs**; (vi) demonstrated **predictive validity** of the score on unlabelled post-cutoff images; (vii) an audit of the current generation.

**Mandatory novelty test (three closest papers).**
1. **VISE: Paying More Attention to Visual Tokens in Self-Evolving LMMs** (arXiv 2606.27373). Label-free RL on Qwen3-VL-2B with two rewards: a geometric-invariance reward (predict a box on the original and on an affine / crop / flip transform of the whole image; reward = normalized GIoU between the projected and new boxes) and a semantic-invariance reward (predict a box around the queried object, replace that region by a Gaussian-blurred "ghosted" version with sigma 25 while preserving context, then reward 1 only if the model judges the object visible on the original and absent on the ghosted image); evaluated on captioning (+16.85 CIDEr COCO, +19.66 TextCaps) and CHAIR-I, with no localization metric `[VERIFIED: arXiv 2606.27373 abstract and HTML method section, read 2026-09-02]`. IDEA-91 requires the localization output itself to respond (box moves to a valid fallback or becomes `none`), uses realistic removal paired with control edits so that "region degraded" carries no information, tests contextual rather than geometric invariance, adds sufficiency and text-side controls, validates against natural pairs and human labels, and audits existing models; VISE's two rewards are arm (b) of the direct comparison and the baseline for `V`.
2. **DeFacto: Counterfactual Thinking with Images** (arXiv 2509.20912). Builds positive / counterfactual / random-mask variants from a language-guided evidence pipeline and trains GRPO with an evidence-consistency reward for VQA answers, with DeFacto-1.5K `[VERIFIED: abstract]`. IDEA-91 conditions realistic edits on the model's own predicted region at reward time and requires the localization output to respond; the object under test is the box, not the answer; DeFacto's gray mask is arm (a).
3. **HalluSegBench / RobustSeg** (arXiv 2506.21546). Counterfactual edits of *ground-truth* referents; segment in the factual image, abstain in the counterfactual; SFT-based counterfactual fine-tuning, 30% hallucination reduction `[VERIFIED: abstract, V-075]`. IDEA-91 anchors on predictions rather than ground truth, adds invariance, `N+` and sufficiency, needs no labels, validates against natural pairs, and uses the relations as a metric with predictive validity.

**Further one-sentence deltas (required by the lead and the Skeptic).**
- **PAPO** (arXiv 2507.06448): an Implicit Perception Loss in the form of a KL-divergence term inside GRPO/DAPO, no extra data, reward model or teacher; +4.4 to 17.5% on multimodal reasoning, 30.5% fewer perception errors `[VERIFIED: abstract]`; the original-versus-corrupted-image construction of that KL is a body-level detail `[PARTIALLY per Verifier: not in the abstract; Skeptic index]`; IDEA-91 targets localization outputs with region-specific edits and abstention semantics; PAPO is arm (d).

## 8. Main risks and weaknesses

1. **Artifact leakage** (most likely reviewer objection). Mitigation: always-edited design, K1 with a strong forensic adversary on both hole types, behavioural gate, invariance monitor during training, natural pairs in every table.
2. **Editors fail on small, cluttered referents**, which are the hard cases. Mitigation: report judge-verified edit success by referent size and the fraction of each benchmark the training half can use; small referents are evaluated with natural pairs and text-side operators, which need no editing.
3. **Necessity measures reliance, not correctness.** Mitigation: `S` carries correctness, `N+` requires a valid fallback; K3 validates the conjunction against people before any benchmark claim.
4. **Cheap counterfactuals may match.** Mitigation: the pre-registered direct arm K5 now includes VISE's blur-ghosting; the audit and metric stand regardless.
5. **Reward hacking**: always-abstain, moving the box to satisfy the judge, or learning judge A's quirks. Mitigation: positive-rate constraint; judge on crops that include `supp(q)`; held-out judge B and human `S`.
6. **Multi-referent expressions.** Mitigation: the duplicates rule (REDUNDANT, set-level necessity) and `N+`; UNRESOLVED items excluded.
7. **SAM 3 errors propagate into `supp(q)` and the bank.** Mitigation: SAM 3 recall reported on the validated subset; `IC` sensitivity band from a second detector.
8. **GroundLive circularity and salience bias.** Mitigation: different-lineage generation, difficulty stratification matched to GroundingME, per-stratum `S` against the human anchor.
9. **Reviewer reads it as "VISE with boxes" or "DeFacto for boxes".** Pre-empt: the first two contributions train nothing; the training section's headline is the direct arm in which VISE and DeFacto are arms, not a new reward.

## 10. Paper pitch (Phase 3, Researcher3, 2026-09-02)

**Working title.** *Right Box, Wrong Reason: Interventional Verification of Localization in Vision-Language Models.* (Fallback if the audit is a null result: *Interventional Consistency: a Label-Free, Contamination-Resistant Score for Visual Grounding.*)

**Abstract-style claim (one paragraph; bracketed numbers are predictions, Section 6, `[SPECULATION]`).** Vision-language models are scored on grounding by IoU against a reference box, which cannot tell a model that found the referent from one that found the only salient object, the largest instance of the head noun, or the location its training data favours. We treat a predicted region as a hypothesis and test it by editing the image at that region: the localization output must not move when the image is edited elsewhere (invariance), must move to a valid fallback or become `none` when the referent is removed (necessity), must be confirmable from the region alone (sufficiency), and must respond to the expression (text-side controls). Every removal is paired with an artifact-matched control edit, so no relation can be passed by detecting the edit, and every edited result is reported next to real present/absent image pairs. Auditing twelve current grounders and grounded-reasoning models on uncontaminated benchmarks, we find that [10 to 25]% of IoU-correct boxes from plain grounders and [25 to 45]% from box-in-trace reasoners fail necessity or invariance on human-validated items: the box was right and the model was not using it. The resulting label-free score, interventional consistency (`IC`), agrees with human judgment, predicts per-item correctness on Ref-L4 and GroundingME better than the crop judge alone and better than a two-seed disagreement predictor, and, computed on unlabelled post-cutoff images, tracks labelled hard-benchmark accuracy across model units, which gives grounding its first benchmark that does not expire when it is trained on. Used as a GRPO reward on COCO-free images, the same relations raise GroundingME rejection from about 0 to [40 to 60]% without loss of positive accuracy, and a matched-compute comparison against gray-mask, blur-ghosting, attention-mask and corrupted-image-KL counterfactuals shows that only realistic edits with control edits transfer to natural pairs [or: shows no advantage, and the training section is dropped].

**The three contributions a reviewer would list.**
1. The first prediction-anchored causal audit of *localization outputs* across the current generation (plain grounders, box-in-trace reasoners, crop models, a specialist), with the right-box-wrong-reason rate as a new quantity, validated against 1,000 human labels and reported side by side on edited and natural pairs, with a memorization-probe row for RefCOCO. This is the 2018 Cirik question and the 2020 Shrestha control finally run on boxes.
2. `IC`, a label-free grounding score with demonstrated predictive validity: item-level AUROC that beats the judge alone and self-consistency (91-E6, 91-E7), unit-level correlation with labelled hard-benchmark accuracy under a cluster bootstrap (91-E4), and GroundLive, a refreshable post-cutoff evaluation generator released as a generator rather than a set.
3. (Conditional on K5.) The pre-registered matched-compute comparison of five counterfactual generators inside GRPO, and the realistic-edit reward when it wins on natural pairs and GroundingME rejection. If it does not win, the comparison is reported as a negative result in the appendix and contributions 1 and 2 carry the paper.
