# Data and compute scale in small-VLM grounding, rejection and GRPO papers (fact-check, 2026-10-03)

Question: how much training data and compute do papers use when they fine-tune a 2B-8B multimodal LLM for referring expression comprehension, in particular with rejection of no-target expressions and / or GRPO-style RL?
Method: full text extracted from arXiv HTML and PDF (pypdf) for every paper; CVF PDFs for ROD-MLLM and the Perceval supplement. 14 requested papers plus 7 additions (15a-15g).

Tags on every number:
- `[§x]`, `[Tab. x]`, `[App. x]`, `[abstract]` = verified, read at that place in the paper.
- `n.s.` = not stated in the paper.
- `[derived]` = my arithmetic on verified numbers.
- `[repo]`, `[3rd-party]` = unverified against the paper (seen only in the code repo or in another paper).
- Numbers in the sections below the table repeat table entries and keep their tags unless tagged again.

arXiv ids: every id supplied is correct. ViSurf is arXiv 2510.10606. Versions read: RC-GRPO v1, GroundingME v2, RefBench-PRO v3, RexSeek v2, VLM-R1 v2, ViSurf v4, DeepEyes v3, Ground-R1 v3, TreeVGR v2, all others v1.

## Checks and corrections

- **RC-GRPO: verified** (RL-only, 2,000 FineCops-Ref samples split 1,000 / 1,000, 500 steps, LoRA, 2xA800). Three qualifiers: 500 steps is per stage and RC-GRPO-II runs two stages on the same data; "2xA800" sits in the SFT clause and GRPO hardware is not given separately; LoRA rank and LR are not given. 2,000 samples over 500 steps is 4 prompts per step [derived], which the paper does not reconcile with its "batch size 8".
- **GroundingME: one correction.** Each SFT set has 30,000 instances in total, sampled from the 30k + 30k pool; it is not 30k positives plus negatives. 3 epochs. Rejection 27.9 is right and belongs to the 2:1 run (20k negatives + 10k positives [derived]).
- **Ref-R1: verified** (180K SFT + 80k RL). RL does not raise Reject: 61.50 after SFT-CoT, 58.20 after RL.
- **Rex-Thinker: verified** (90,824 traces). GRPO runs 0.25 epoch of the same set and stops when the reward plateaus [App. A.2.2]; group 8; steps not stated; 112 h on 8xA100.
- **DeepEyes compute.** The "32 H100, 48 h, 32 epochs" figure comes from TreeVGR §4.1, not from DeepEyes, whose paper says 80 iterations x 256 prompts.
- **Perceval.** Neither the paper nor the CVPR supplement (prompts only) gives a data size for the checker or the generator.

## What scale is typical

- **SFT stage.** A task-specific SFT stage uses 12.5k to 180k samples: CRS 12.5k, GroundingME 30k x 3 epochs, TreeVGR 35k, UniVG-R1 90k, Rex-Thinker 90.8k x 2 epochs, RexSeek 103k (last stage), Ref-R1 180K. Models built for grounding from the start use 0.4M to 22M (Ground-V, ROD-MLLM, Motto, Rex-Omni). SFT sets near 2k appear only as a baseline (RC-GRPO, where P-acc fell 73.6 -> 30.1 [Tab. 2]) or as an ablation point (CRS Tab. X).
- **RL stage.** RL sets run from 2k to 80k prompts, median about 10k [derived]: RC-GRPO 2k, Perception-R1 5k, ViSurf 7.2k, Ground-R1 8k draws, VG-Refiner 9K, Group Revision 9k, UniVG-R1 10k, DeepEyes 20.5k draws, Rex-Thinker about 22.7k, TreeVGR 37K, Rex-Omni 66K, Ref-R1 80k. Visual-RFT is the few-shot outlier (8 to 239 images).
- **RL recipe.** Group size is 8 in the 9 papers that state it, and 16 in DeepEyes. RL learning rate is 1e-6 in all 9 papers that state it. Most runs make one pass or less over the RL set (RC-GRPO one per stage, VG-Refiner 1, Ref-R1 1 [derived], Rex-Thinker 0.25, DeepEyes 0.44 [derived]); TreeVGR (5 epochs) and Visual-RFT (200-500 steps on at most 239 images) are the exceptions. Stated step counts: 200-500 (Visual-RFT), 500 per stage (RC-GRPO), at least 600 (VLM-R1, last reported checkpoint), 1,000 (Ground-R1), 5,000 (Ref-R1).
- **LoRA vs full.** Of the 21 papers, 3 state LoRA (RC-GRPO, rank not given; ROD-MLLM, r128; Ground-V's LISA, rank not given), 4 state full or LLM-only training (Rex-Thinker, RexSeek, Motto, Rex-Omni), and 14 do not say. Among the 14 requested the split is 1 / 2 / 11.
- **Compute.** One 8-GPU node is the norm where stated (Ref-R1, Rex-Thinker, Ground-R1, Ground-V, Rex-Omni's RL stage, and TreeVGR plus 8 more for its judge). RC-GRPO (2xA800) and VG-Refiner (4xA100) are the small end; Motto (32xA800) and Rex-Omni's SFT (64xA100, 8 days) the large end. Stated RL wall-clock: 12 h (Ground-R1), 24 h (Rex-Omni), 112 h (Rex-Thinker).
- **Negatives for rejection.** No-target samples number 200 (ViSurf), 1,000 (RC-GRPO), 2,500 (CRS), 3.3k-20k (GroundingME [derived]), 13.3k (RexSeek), at most 49k (Ref-R1 pool), 63k (Ground-V). Across papers the rejection score on each paper's own test does not track that count: RexSeek 54.1 (13.3k), ViSurf 57.1 (200) and 80.2 (500), Ref-R1 58.2, CRS 58.6 on InternVL2.5-8B but 27.1 on Qwen2-VL-7B (same 2,500), RC-GRPO 61.1 on 7B (1,000). The one transfer test to a harder benchmark reached 27.9 (GroundingME).
- **Evidence on small data.** Three papers vary the size. RC-GRPO, RL on 0.5K / 1K / 2K / 5K: Pr 63.0 / 65.6 / 66.6 / 65.4; at 0.5K the model over-rejects (P-acc 58.7 against 73.6 for the base, N-acc 75.9) [Tab. 7]. ViSurf, 50 / 200 / 500 negatives: N-Acc 44.4 / 57.1 / 80.2 [Tab. 9]. UniVG-R1: CoT-SFT on 21k vs 90k gives 64.50 vs 69.00, and RL alone on 21k gives 57.43 [Tab. 4].
- **Where 550 SFT items + 320 RL prompts x 8 rollouts, 39 steps, 4B, LoRA r16 sits** (all ratios [derived]):
  - SFT: 3.6x below the smallest SFT set in the table (RC-GRPO's 2k baseline), 23x below the smallest SFT stage a method relies on (CRS), and 64x to 330x below the SFT stages of the four SFT -> RL papers (35K to 180K).
  - RL prompts: just above Visual-RFT's few-shot range (8 to 239 images), 1.6x below RC-GRPO's smallest ablation point (0.5K), 6x below its main run, and 16x to 250x below the rest.
  - Updates: 39 steps is 5x to 13x fewer than Visual-RFT and RC-GRPO (200-500 steps), the two papers closest in data size. Total rollouts are 2,560 against 16,000 per stage in RC-GRPO.
  - Base and recipe: the base size matches RC-GRPO's Qwen3-VL-4B and group size 8 is standard. LoRA r16 cannot be placed, because the only reported rank is 128.
