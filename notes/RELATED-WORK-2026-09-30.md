## Implications for our claims (2026-09-30)
- Label injection (our "inject-gt both") is the same core idea as ViSurf's ground-truth rollout, and RC-GRPO's forced rejection rollouts; cite both, it is not our contribution.
- Per-candidate verification CoT with abstention exists (Rex-Thinker). Our differences to argue: isolated audits per candidate with zoom-in views, per-clause written observations (anti-echo), the rule-derived answer, the dense sycophancy penalty, and the target task (one false detail in a long description, not person referring).
- Directly comparable numbers without reimplementation: RC-GRPO on FineCops-Ref test and gRefCOCO (same Qwen3-VL-4B base); PR-Bench Rejection (Qwen3-VL-8B 16.2, Motto-2B 46.9); OpenRef N3R (Qwen3-VL-4B 37.9); Ref-Adv-s (Qwen3-VL-4B 41.9).

## Prior-art sweep for our novelty claims (2026-09-30 afternoon; web, mostly abstracts; venues marked "per arXiv comments" unverified)
Closest overall: **RC-GRPO (2608.04698)** - same Qwen3-VL-4B, one-component-perturbed negatives (FineCops-Ref), forced "None" injected into groups, alpha scaling with a Pareto plot (claims the knobs *improve* the frontier, contradicting our finding 5c), SFT-vs-RL comparison (SFT with negatives: N-acc 95.4 but P-acc 73.6 -> 30.1; also reports GRPO drifting toward None), stage II reward for naming the falsified pattern. No candidates / zoom / isolation / per-claim observations.
- Isolated per-candidate verification at inference already exists: Zero-Shot REC via True/False Verification (2509.09958, arXiv; each proposal alone, separate call, "reduces cross-box interference"; abstention); DRAgent (2608.22885, arXiv); GroundingAgent (2511.19516, AAAI 2026 uncertain; describes each candidate with the query withheld = blind observe-first); GUI-Actor (2506.03143, NeurIPS 2025); sCoT (2512.08511, isolated subagents, loss-masked, VQA). Not found: a *trained* line-by-line "claim | seen | verdict" audit in isolated contexts inside one RL policy with per-audit advantages.
- CoVe (2309.11495) = Findings of ACL 2024; its factored variant is motivated by verifiers "copying or repeating" the draft -> text ancestor of our echo finding.
- Echo / agreement bias: SGV "Let's Think in Two Steps" (2507.11662, ICLR 2026; MLLM verifiers over-validate; fix = generate expectations before seeing the item, prompting only); "Are VLMs Seeing or Just Saying?" (2605.15864, ICML 2026 oral per arXiv); video-LLM citation check restating the claim (2608.15574); self-correction mirage in VLM grounding (2606.13156).
- Data / reward side: ViCrit (2506.10128, NeurIPS 2025; one subtle injected error in captions, reward for locating it); RA-RFT (2511.23151, CVPR 2026 per third party; refusal for hard-irrelevant queries in video grounding); Perceval (2604.24583, CVPR 2026; claim-level checks + token-level advantage penalties on hallucinated spans = precedent for our dense penalty); PACG (2609.36572).
- SFT vs RL for refusal: Kang et al. (2403.05612, NAACL 2025; SFT targets on unfamiliar examples set the default answer = closest concept to finding 5b); Safety Mirage (2503.11832); Hallucination Tax (2505.13988, Findings EMNLP 2025); SFT Memorizes, RL Generalizes (ICML 2025); Abstain-R1 (2604.17073).
- Observe-first / perception rewards: Vision-SR1 (2508.19652), Visionary-R1, Perception-R1, PAPO, TreeVGR (ICLR 2026). Claim decomposition: EVPV, SG-PVR, DSG (ICLR 2024). Copy-token loss masking: Hapax (2511.05743). Tool RL / credit: DeepEyes, MGPO, ViGoRL, Chain-of-Focus, VISTA-R1, OP-HRG, ReVISE, SPO, turn-level credit; GT injection: ViSurf, LUFFY.
- Verdict per claim: (1) proposal + conditions: covered; (2) isolation: covered at inference, trained per-claim observation audits in isolation look new; (3) rule-derived answer: covered; (4) each training piece covered, combination new; (5a) echo inside per-claim visual audits and the fix by supervised observations: moderately novel; (5b) same falsified data in SFT vs RL with the clause-type prior: likely novel, must address RC-GRPO's GRPO drift; (5c) contested by RC-GRPO -> needs a head-to-head on the same backbone or drop as a headline claim.
